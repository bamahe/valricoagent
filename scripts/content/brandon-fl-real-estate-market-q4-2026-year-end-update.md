Where July and August data showed sluggish days on market, rising price reduction rates, and buyers gaining leverage for the first time since 2019, October and November data tell a more nuanced story: inventory is stabilizing, well-priced homes are moving faster than expected, and sellers who have adjusted to the new rate environment are closing deals. ## Brandon FL Market Snapshot: Q4 2026

The Brandon market, spanning ZIP codes 33510, 33511, and the border 33596, produced approximately 868 single-family home sales over the trailing twelve months through September 2026, according to data from Horizon Palm Realty's MLS analysis. Active inventory across the core two Brandon ZIPs reached approximately 265 to 320 listings combined as of early November. Pending sales numbered roughly 104, suggesting absorption continues despite the elevated rate environment.

| Metric | Q2 2026 (Summer) | Q4 2026 (Current) | Change |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|--------|
| Median sale price (overall Brandon) | $350,000-$380,000 | $365,000-$390,000 | +2%-4% |
| Price per sq ft | $180-$205 | $185-$210 | Stable |
| Active listings | ~245-280 | ~265-320 | +8%-15% |
| Days on market | 38-55 days | 27-35 days | Faster |
| Months of supply | 3.5-4.5 months | 4.0-4.7 months | Slight increase |
| Price reduction rate | 28-33% | 30-37% | Higher |

The key headline from this table is counterintuitive: active inventory grew slightly while days on market fell. That dynamic typically points to one thing -- pricing discipline. The homes closing in October and November are the ones priced correctly from day one or adjusted in September before the Q4 slowdown hit. The listings sitting at 60 to 90-plus days are largely overpriced relative to current mortgage rate conditions.

Mortgage rates remain the dominant variable. As of early November 2026, 30-year conventional rates are running 6.75 to 7.25 percent for well-qualified buyers. That rate environment does not kill Brandon demand, but it filters it. Buyers are calculating their monthly payments carefully and walking away from homes where sellers have not adjusted asking prices to reflect the current financing cost.

## Brandon by ZIP Code: Q4 2026 Data

### 33510 (Northern Brandon): Entry-Point Market Finds Its Floor

The 33510 ZIP covering northern Brandon showed the most price movement of any Brandon segment this year. Movoto's October data reports a median list price near $345,000, with approximately 134 homes actively listed. Momentum Realty's October analysis tracked a median sale price of approximately $340,000 to $360,000 for closed sales in the trailing 90 days, with days on market settling around 27 days for homes priced in line with comparables.

The year-over-year trajectory in 33510 has been flat to slightly negative in nominal terms. Homes in this ZIP were overpriced relative to condition and stock age during the 2022 to 2023 rate-shock period. That correction is now largely complete. Buyers in 33510 today are finding a market where:

- Homes in the $285,000 to $355,000 range are moving in 25 to 35 days when priced correctly
- Seller concessions of 2% to 3% of purchase price are routine, particularly for older housing stock (1970s to 1980s construction)
- The entry-point buyer pool is thin because first-time buyers at this price range are still stretched by rates; investor demand from cash buyers and landlords is absorbing some of the slack

The floor in 33510 appears to be holding in the low-to-mid $300s for standard condition homes on typical lots. Properties needing substantial HVAC, roof, or electrical work are trading at $20,000 to $35,000 discounts to move-in-ready comparables -- a wider gap than existed in 2021 or 2022.

### 33511 (Central and Southern Brandon): The Core Market Stabilizes

Central Brandon's 33511 ZIP entered Q4 with the most balanced supply and demand of any Brandon segment. Active inventory sits at approximately 200 to 210 listings, with approximately 60 to 70 pending. That ratio translates to roughly 3.0 to 3.5 months of supply in the active price bands ($350,000 to $425,000), which is the tightest segment in core Brandon.

Momentum Realty's November data shows a median list price of approximately $385,000 for 33511, with median days on market around 31 days. Closed sale pricing for October 2026 tracks in the $365,000 to $395,000 range depending on neighborhood quality, school zone, and condition.

The standout performers in 33511 this fall are homes in the Bloomingdale High School zone on lots of 7,500 square feet or larger in move-in condition. These properties are absorbing in under 25 days when priced at or below $395,000. Heather Lakes, Providence Lakes, and the Buckhorn corridor continue to hold premium positioning within 33511.

**Notable 33511 market dynamics in Q4 2026:**

- Buyers with VA financing have made up an increased share of closings -- approximately 15 to 18 percent of recent 33511 closings used a VA loan, partly driven by MacDill AFB-related demand and VA loans' ability to tolerate higher seller concessions without appraisal complications
- Investment activity has picked up modestly in the $310,000 to $360,000 range, with investors underwriting at 5.5 to 6.5 percent gross yields and accepting thin cash-on-cash returns in exchange for Hillsborough County appreciation expectations
- The condo segment (a small fraction of Brandon's market) has seen wider concessions and longer DOM, consistent with statewide condo headwinds from reserve requirement legislation

### 33596 (Bloomingdale / Brandon-Valrico Border): The Premium Zone Holds

The 33596 ZIP, which straddles the Brandon-Valrico border and includes River Hills, Diamond Hill, Buckhorn, and much of the Bloomingdale community, has held its pricing floor better than either core Brandon ZIP. This is a function of the Newsome High School and Bloomingdale High School zone demand, which remains inelastic regardless of rate environment.

As of early November 2026, 33596 median pricing sits in the $460,000 to $480,000 range, essentially flat to the summer 2026 level. Active days on market for 33596 are running 35 to 50 days, which is slower than the 33511 core but faster than 33510. Inventory has ticked up modestly but has not reached the levels that would genuinely shift negotiating power to buyers.

The passage of Florida Amendment 3 on November 3, 2026 adds a specific tailwind to 33596. At a $470,000 assessed value, the 2027 non-school property tax savings under Amendment 3 are approximately $1,100 per year. The [Amendment 3 impact for Valrico homeowners](/blog/florida-amendment-3-passed-november-2026-valrico-tax-savings/) covers the full math by ZIP code.

## Year-End Opportunity: What Q4 2026 Means for Brandon Buyers

November and December in the Brandon market have historically been the most favorable months for buyers -- and 2026 is consistent with that pattern. The specific factors creating opportunity right now:

**1. Sellers who have been listed since August or September are fatigued.**

A home that entered the market in August 2026 at an aspirational price, took price reductions in September, and is still active in November represents a seller who is now genuinely motivated. These are not test-the-water sellers. They have carrying costs, life plans in motion, and in many cases, a move contingent on this sale. That motivation translates into real negotiating room on price, closing costs, or both.

**2. Rate buydowns are more available now than at any point in 2024 or 2025.**

In the current environment, it is routine to request a 1-point to 2-point temporary rate buydown from the seller as part of the closing cost credit. On a $375,000 Brandon home with a 1-2-3 buydown, the buyer pays roughly 4.75% in year one, 5.75% in year two, and 6.75% in year three. This reduces monthly payment friction in the early years when most rate-sensitive buyers are most stretched.

**3. Amendment 3 creates a December 31 deadline with real dollar value.**

Buyers who close by December 31, 2026 establish homestead eligibility for the 2027 tax year. That means the first year of Amendment 3 savings -- approximately $1,000 to $1,200 per year for a typical Brandon or Valrico home -- starts immediately. Buyers who close in January 2027 miss that first year entirely. See the [year-end buyer action plan](/blog/valrico-fl-year-end-buyer-action-plan-close-before-december-31-2026/) for the step-by-step timeline.

**4. Spring competition will return.**

The buyers who sit out November and December will return in March. When they do, inventory in the competitive $350,000 to $420,000 range will face renewed pressure, price reductions will thin out, and the leverage buyers have today will compress. The 2026 Q4 window is real. Buyers who understand this are acting on it.

## Year-End Reality for Brandon Sellers

Sellers listing in Q4 face a tougher path than those who listed in spring, but it is not impossible to close well before year-end if the fundamentals are right.

The two non-negotiables in a Q4 Brandon sale:

**Price correctly from day one.** The rate environment means buyers are running precise monthly payment calculations. A home listed at $395,000 when comparables are trading at $375,000 will sit. A home listed at $375,000 or $378,000 that shows well will generate multiple showings in the first two weeks. The best Q4 sellers are pricing slightly below the comp-based range, not at the top of it.

**Show-ready condition.** November buyers are serious. They are not attending open houses on a lark. They want to make a decision. A home with deferred maintenance, strong pet odors, or dated bathrooms that hasn't been touched since 2015 will not close at peak pricing. Sellers who do the basics -- paint, clean carpet, update light fixtures, landscaping -- see a measurable difference in days to offer.

If you need to sell in Brandon before year-end, contact a local agent immediately. The time to list is now, not Thanksgiving week.

## Brandon vs. Valrico: Where Are We Heading Into 2027?

The Brandon-Valrico price gap has narrowed slightly but not closed. The relationship that existed in summer 2026 persists:

| Market | Median Price Range | Key Driver |
|--------|-------------------|------------|
| Brandon 33510 | $325,000-$360,000 | Entry point, proximity |
| Brandon 33511 | $365,000-$395,000 | Established neighborhoods |
| Brandon/Valrico 33596 | $460,000-$480,000 | School zone premium |
| Valrico 33594 | $370,000-$385,000 | Bloomingdale, good value |
| Valrico 33596 | $460,000-$480,000 | Newsome HS zone |

The pricing logic heading into 2027 is straightforward: buyers who prioritize convenience and commute access will continue to find Brandon's 33510 and 33511 ZIPs the most affordable entry points in east Hillsborough County. Buyers who prioritize school zoning and are willing to pay a premium for Newsome or Bloomingdale High School access will continue to cluster in 33596 regardless of whether the address says Brandon or Valrico.

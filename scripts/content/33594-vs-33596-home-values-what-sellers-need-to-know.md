## Two ZIP Codes, Two Markets

Valrico spans ZIP codes 33594 and 33596, and they perform differently in nearly every metric. For sellers, understanding which zip you are in — and how it affects your pricing, buyer pool, and marketing strategy — is the difference between a fast sale at fair value and a frustrating listing that sits.

## The Numbers Side by Side

| Metric | 33594 (Western Valrico) | 33596 (Eastern Valrico) |
|---|---|---|
| Median sale price | $365K to $425K | $465K to $515K |
| Price per sq ft | $185 to $210 | $210 to $240 |
| Average days on market | 30 to 42 days | 25 to 38 days |
| Buyer profile | Value-conscious, first-timers | Family-focused, school-driven |
| Primary school zone | Bloomingdale High | Newsome High |
| HOA prevalence | Lower — many no-HOA options | Higher — more community HOAs |
| Typical lot size | 8,000 to 12,000 sq ft | 6,000 to 10,000 sq ft (except Diamond Hill) |

The $75K to $100K median gap between the two zip codes is driven primarily by school zoning. Newsome High School in 33596 attracts family buyers willing to pay a premium for the school assignment. This single factor accounts for the majority of the pricing difference.

## Selling in 33594 — Western Valrico

### Key Neighborhoods
Bloomingdale, Twin Lakes, Brentwood Hills, Bloomingdale Oaks, Bloomingdale East, Bloomingdale Cove

### Your Buyer Profile
Buyers in 33594 are value-oriented. They are comparing your home to similar options in Brandon, which sits immediately to the west. Price sensitivity is higher here than in 33596 because buyers have viable alternatives at lower price points just a few miles away.

First-time buyers and investors are particularly active in 33594. FHA and VA buyers represent a larger share of the pool than in 33596.

### Pricing Strategy
Price at or slightly below comparable sales. In 33594, overpricing does not create "room for negotiation" — it pushes buyers to Brandon alternatives. The homes that sell fastest in this zip code are priced competitively from day one, in move-in ready condition.

Pull comps from your specific subdivision section, not all of 33594. Bloomingdale East prices differently than original Bloomingdale. Bloomingdale Oaks has its own pricing tier. Twin Lakes operates as its own micro-market.

### Marketing Emphasis
Lead with value and space. Your buyers are looking for more home per dollar than they can find in Brandon. Emphasize lot size (generally larger than newer construction), mature landscaping, no CDD, and proximity to Brandon shopping and dining.

If the home has been updated (kitchen, bathrooms, flooring), lead with the updates. Condition is the primary differentiator in 33594 because the locations are comparable.

## Selling in 33596 — Eastern Valrico

### Key Neighborhoods
Buckhorn, Buckhorn Preserve, River Hills, Diamond Hill, Canterbury Oaks, and smaller subdivisions east of Valrico Road

### Your Buyer Profile
Buyers in 33596 are school-driven. The majority are families with school-age children specifically seeking Newsome High School zoning. Many are relocating from other parts of Tampa Bay or from out of state. They have done their school research and Newsome is on their list before they ever contact an agent.

These buyers will stretch their budget for the right home in the right school zone. Price sensitivity exists, but it is moderated by the limited inventory in the Newsome zone.

### Pricing Strategy
Price accurately based on subdivision-level comps, but do not undervalue the school zone premium. A 4/2 in Buckhorn with similar condition to a 4/2 in Bloomingdale is worth $50K to $80K more — and the data consistently supports this premium.

For River Hills and Diamond Hill ($550K+), price based on the specific micro-market. These are small-inventory neighborhoods where each sale significantly influences the next. I pull every closed sale, pending sale, and active listing in these communities before recommending a price.

### Marketing Emphasis
Lead with school zoning. "Newsome High School zone" should be in the first line of your listing description and in every social media advertisement. This is the keyword that drives buyer clicks in 33596.

For River Hills: emphasize gated security, lot size, golf course access, and exclusivity.
For Diamond Hill: emphasize land, no HOA, privacy, and Newsome zoning.
For Buckhorn: emphasize community amenities, family-friendly environment, and Newsome zoning.

I run targeted digital advertising for 33596 listings specifically reaching families relocating to Hillsborough County who have searched for "Newsome High School" or "best schools Valrico FL." These are your highest-probability buyers.

## When 33594 Sellers Compete With 33596

Occasionally, sellers in 33594 try to price at 33596 levels because the homes are physically close. A Bloomingdale home on the eastern edge of 33594 might be 2 miles from a Buckhorn home in 33596. Same square footage, same condition — but a $50K+ price gap.

The difference is school zoning and buyer perception. Listing agents who do not understand this dynamic overprice 33594 homes and then wonder why they are not getting showings. The buyer searching for "Valrico homes, Newsome zone" filters out your 33594 listing before they ever see it.

If you are in 33594, price to compete with Brandon and attract value-focused buyers. Do not price to compete with 33596 and attract nobody.

## The Portability Factor

Sellers who have been in their homes for 10+ years may have significant Save Our Homes cap benefits — their assessed value is well below market value, resulting in lower property taxes. When they sell and buy a new home, the assessed value resets to market value, potentially increasing their tax bill by $1,000 to $2,000+ per year.

Florida's portability provision allows you to transfer the cap benefit to a new home within the state (up to $500K of benefit). If you are selling a long-term home in 33594 and buying in 33596 (or vice versa), portability can soften the tax increase. File for portability with the Property Appraiser within the required timeframe.

## Which ZIP Code Appreciates Faster?

Both zip codes have shown steady long-term appreciation. 33596 has slightly outperformed 33594 during strong markets (2020-2022) because school-driven demand intensifies when rates are low and purchasing power is high. During corrections (2023-2024), both zips pulled back similarly.

Over a 10-year holding period, the difference in appreciation rates between the two zip codes is modest — 1 to 2% annually. The bigger factor in your individual return is buying and selling price relative to condition and neighborhood, not zip code level trends.

## Largo FL Real Estate Market Overview 2026

The Largo FL real estate market in 2026 is in a measured cooling phase. [Redfin's Largo city-level data](https://www.redfin.com/city/9116/FL/Largo) shows a median sale price of approximately $319,950 to $325,528 for Q3 2026, down approximately 4.9% year over year. That figure blends all property types including condos, townhomes, and single-family detached homes across Largo's multiple ZIP codes.

The directional trend is consistent with broader Pinellas County patterns. Inventory has risen from the 2021 to 2022 lows. Months of supply city-wide sits at approximately 3.5, which puts Largo in balanced-to-slightly-buyer-favorable territory rather than the deep buyer's market visible in the St. Pete condo segment. Sellers who price to current comps are closing. Sellers who are anchored to 2022 or 2023 prices are sitting on their listings.

Days on market has lengthened across most Largo ZIP codes in 2026. The city-wide average for single-family homes is running approximately 45 to 70 days from list to contract, up from the 15 to 25 day windows of 2021 and 2022. This shift represents an enormous improvement in buyer leverage compared to the pandemic-era market.

## ZIP Code Breakdown: Largo by the Numbers

Largo spans six primary ZIP codes with meaningfully different price points, housing stock ages, and buyer dynamics. Here is the current picture.

### 33771 (East Largo, US-19 Corridor)

The 33771 ZIP covers the eastern portion of Largo along and near the US-19 commercial corridor. This ZIP includes a mix of older single-family neighborhoods from the 1960s and 1970s and a significant condominium inventory. The median sale price in 33771 is running approximately $270,000 to $290,000 for single-family homes, with condos pulling the blended figure lower.

Buyers who want to be inside Largo city limits at the lowest entry price point, with access to Pinellas Trail, Largo Central Park, and the US-19 retail corridor, will find the most options in 33771. Days on market is running approximately 50 to 65 days, giving buyers meaningful time to perform due diligence without being rushed.

### 33773 (Central Largo, Keene Road Area)

The 33773 ZIP is one of the more active Largo submarkets and covers a broad swath of central Largo including neighborhoods south of Ulmerton Road and east of Keene Road. Redfin data shows a median sale price of approximately $295,000 for 33773, down approximately 4.4% year over year. Days on market for 33773 is averaging approximately 53 days.

This ZIP includes a good mix of 1970s through 1990s single-family homes on larger lots, along with several mobile home and manufactured housing communities that pull the blended median lower. Buyers looking for a detached single-family home in 33773 at the $270,000 to $320,000 price range have legitimate options in 2026.

### 33774 (Southwest Largo, Seminole Adjacent)

The 33774 ZIP covers southwest Largo near the Seminole border, including neighborhoods that sit within a short drive of Indian Rocks Beach. This is the premium tier of the Largo market. The median sale price in 33774 is approximately $375,000 to $400,000, reflecting the higher concentration of renovated mid-century ranch homes, newer construction on scraped lots, and proximity to the Gulf coast beach communities.

Days on market in 33774 is running approximately 60 to 70 days. Buyers in this ZIP are typically comparing options with Seminole and the beach communities directly, so the pool of active, well-informed buyers is higher than in central Largo. The higher days on market reflects more deliberate buyer behavior at this price tier, not a distressed market.

The 33774 corridor along Seminole Boulevard includes established commercial activity and several recently renovated residential neighborhoods. This is where the REMAX Collective Largo office operates, which gives our team firsthand visibility into inventory and buyer activity in this corridor.

### 33778 (South Largo, Pinellas Park Adjacent)

The 33778 ZIP occupies the southern portion of Largo near the Pinellas Park border. This ZIP has the most diverse housing mix in Largo, including mobile home communities, manufactured housing, and standard site-built single-family homes. Redfin data shows a median of approximately $157,500 for 33778, though that figure is heavily influenced by the volume of mobile and manufactured home transactions.

For buyers specifically targeting site-built single-family homes in 33778, the realistic price range is $255,000 to $330,000 depending on condition, size, and specific location within the ZIP. Investors who are comfortable with manufactured housing and understand the land ownership structure can find higher gross yields in 33778 than anywhere else in Largo.

### 33776 and 33777 (West and Northwest Largo)

These ZIPs cover the western and northwestern sections of Largo, with 33776 including neighborhoods west of Missouri Avenue and closer to the Pinellas Trail system. Median prices in these ZIPs sit in the $305,000 to $355,000 range for single-family homes. Days on market is averaging 45 to 60 days.

## Largo Condo Market: What's Different in 2026

Like St. Petersburg, Largo has a significant condominium inventory, and the condo segment is experiencing the same structural pressure from Florida's Senate Bill 4-D reserve funding mandate for buildings three stories or taller and more than 25 years old. Sellers in older Largo mid-rise and high-rise buildings are listing rather than absorbing upcoming special assessment costs.

Largo's condo market is less concentrated in luxury waterfront towers than St. Pete, so the SB 4-D pressure is somewhat diluted. However, buyers of any Largo condominium in a building built before 2001 and three or more stories should absolutely verify the milestone inspection status, current reserve funding level, and any pending special assessments before making an offer. These are not negotiable due diligence items; they are essential.

Ground-floor and two-story condo and villa communities in Largo are not subject to the SB 4-D reserve requirements to the same degree and are showing better market velocity than older high-rise inventory.

## Insurance Costs: What Largo Buyers Actually Pay

Largo's proximity to the Gulf of Mexico puts its insurance costs above inland Hillsborough County markets but generally below St. Pete's more waterfront-dense market.

For a non-waterfront single-family home in central Largo (33771, 33773, 33778), a standard HO-3 policy runs approximately $3,200 to $5,000 per year in 2026. Properties closer to Indian Rocks Beach and the Gulf in the 33774 ZIP or near Clearwater Harbor can run materially higher.

Flood zone status varies significantly within Largo. Portions of 33774 and 33771 near bodies of water carry AE designations with mandatory flood insurance requirements under most lender guidelines. Flood insurance under NFIP Risk Rating 2.0 for an AE-designated Largo property runs approximately $1,200 to $3,500 per year depending on the specific elevation certificate and structure.

By comparison, most of Valrico sits in FEMA Flood Zone X with no mandatory flood insurance requirement, and standard HO-3 premiums run approximately $2,800 to $4,200 per year. The insurance carrying cost difference between Largo and inland east Hillsborough markets like Valrico is real and meaningful over a 10-year hold. Always get current insurance quotes before finalizing an offer on any Largo property.

For flood zone verification, use FEMA's Flood Map Service Center at [msc.fema.gov](https://msc.fema.gov) on any individual address before writing an offer. The [Pinellas County Property Appraiser](https://www.pcpao.gov) is the official source for property valuation and assessment data.

## Property Taxes: Pinellas County vs Hillsborough County

Property taxes represent another material carrying cost difference between Largo and inland markets.

**Pinellas County effective millage:** The effective tax rate for a homesteaded property in Largo is approximately 1.15% to 1.35% of assessed value. On a $320,000 assessed value with homestead exemption, a Largo homeowner pays approximately $2,800 to $3,700 per year.

**Hillsborough County comparison:** Hillsborough County's effective rate runs approximately 1.35% to 1.55% on homesteaded properties. A Valrico homeowner at $413,000 assessed pays approximately $4,500 to $5,500 per year.

The Pinellas County millage is modestly lower than Hillsborough County, though the gap narrows when comparing absolute dollar amounts at different assessed values. At comparable property values, Largo buyers pay slightly less in annual property taxes than Valrico buyers, but the differential is smaller than the gap between Hernando County (Spring Hill) and Hillsborough County. Florida's homestead exemption and 3% Save Our Homes cap apply identically in both counties.

## Schools: Pinellas County School Choice in Largo

Like St. Petersburg, Largo is served by the Pinellas County School District's school choice system. Students are not automatically assigned to a school based on address for most public schools; families submit ranked applications and the district assigns based on availability and sibling priority.

The Largo area is primarily served by Largo High School, which has been subject to a recent [Pinellas County Schools](https://www.pcsb.org) redesignation as a Choice school, offering specialized career and technical education pathways. Clearwater High and Seminole High are choice options within driving distance of some Largo ZIP codes.

This school system structure differs materially from Hillsborough County, where address determines school assignment. Valrico's 33596 ZIP feeds into Newsome High School, which carries a measurable and persistent home price premium. There is no equivalent address-based school zone premium in Largo. Families who want school zone certainty built into their home equity tend to find the Hillsborough zone system more predictable. ## How Does Largo Compare to Valrico for Buyers?

These markets serve different buyer priorities. Here is the direct comparison for buyers weighing both options.

**Price:** Largo's median sits at approximately $319,950 city-wide. Valrico's blended median across both ZIPs is approximately $413,000. Buyers who want coastal Pinellas County access can enter Largo at a meaningfully lower price point than most Valrico options, though the comparison changes when controlling for property type and condition.

**Insurance and flood exposure:** Valrico wins on overall carrying cost. The inland location eliminates flood zone concerns on most properties and keeps homeowners insurance materially lower.

**Commute:** Largo to downtown Tampa runs approximately 25 to 45 minutes via the Courtney Campbell Causeway or SR-60 depending on traffic and starting point. Valrico to downtown Tampa runs approximately 30 to 50 minutes via I-4 and I-275. Commute times are broadly comparable for Tampa-based workers.

**Lifestyle:** Largo offers easy access to Clearwater Beach, Indian Rocks Beach, and the Gulf coast recreational corridor. The Pinellas Trail passes through Largo and connects to a regional system. Largo's density and urban character is more suburban-coastal than Valrico's suburban-inland character. Valrico offers newer housing stock in many subdivisions, established school zone premiums, and direct access to east Hillsborough employment and retail.

**Beach access:** Largo is approximately 10 to 20 minutes from Gulf-front beaches. Valrico is approximately 60 to 90 minutes from the nearest Gulf beaches. For buyers who want to be able to visit the beach on a weekday evening without planning a trip, this is a meaningful quality-of-life difference.

## Q4 2026 Buyer Strategy for Largo

If you are seriously evaluating a Largo purchase in the current market, here is a practical framework for Q4 2026.

**Prioritize 33774 for beach-proximity value.** The southwest Largo ZIP gives buyers the closest beach proximity of any Largo ZIP at prices below the beach communities themselves. Indian Rocks Beach prices start well above Largo 33774 medians for equivalent square footage, making 33774 the value play for buyers who want Gulf coast access without full beach community carrying costs.

**Verify flood zone and insurance before every offer.** Largo's flood map is not uniform. A home two streets from an AE flood zone can be in Zone X, with dramatically different insurance costs. Get the address-specific flood zone verification and a current insurance quote before writing any offer.

**Use the balanced market to negotiate.** At 3.5 months of supply and 45 to 70 days on market, Largo buyers have real leverage. Seller contributions toward closing costs of $5,000 to $8,000 are achievable on appropriately priced listings that have been sitting 45 or more days. Price reductions are active across the market; over 30% of current Largo listings carry a price reduction history.

**Separate condos from single-family homes in your search.** The condo segment is carrying more supply and seller concessions than the single-family market in most Largo ZIPs. If condos are on your consideration list, budget for a full review of the building's milestone inspection status, reserve fund status, and any pending special assessments.

The question comes up constantly among buyers researching the Tampa Bay area: should I buy in Pasco County or Hillsborough County? In practice, that question usually comes down to Wesley Chapel or Land O Lakes versus Valrico, Brandon, or Riverview. Both corridors sit within 30 to 40 miles of downtown Tampa. Both have seen significant growth since 2020. But the markets behave differently, the tradeoffs are real, and the right answer depends on what you are actually optimizing for.

Barrett Henry is a Broker Associate at REMAX Collective with 23 years of real estate experience in east Hillsborough County. His read on the comparison: "Both markets have good inventory right now and buyers have leverage in both. The difference is what you're getting for your money, and the factors driving value appreciation over the next five years."

This guide breaks down the numbers, the neighborhoods, the school options, the commute math, and the lifestyle tradeoffs for buyers deciding between Pasco County and east Hillsborough County in 2027.

## The Price Gap: What the Data Shows

As of late 2026, Pasco County's 12-month median sold price runs approximately $368,000, down 0.7 percent year over year. Hillsborough County's median runs approximately $400,000, with Valrico sitting at approximately $413,000 to $422,000 across its two ZIP codes.

That $30,000 to $55,000 gap has held for years. The underlying reasons are durable: Hillsborough County has better school zone options in its eastern suburbs, more established neighborhoods with mature tree canopy, and faster commute times to Tampa's major employment centers via I-75 and the Selmon Expressway.

**Pasco County price benchmarks (late 2026):**
- Wesley Chapel (33543, 33544, 33545): $380,000 to $450,000 median, depending on community
- Land O Lakes (34638, 34639): $340,000 to $400,000 median
- Lutz (34637, 34638): $380,000 to $430,000 median
- Zephyrhills (33540, 33541, 33542): $280,000 to $330,000 median

**East Hillsborough County price benchmarks (late 2026):**
- Valrico 33594 (Bloomingdale, Twin Lakes, Copper Ridge): $370,000 to $385,000 median
- Valrico 33596 (Buckhorn, River Hills, Diamond Hill, Newsome zone): $460,000 to $475,000 median
- Brandon 33511 (Providence Lakes, Heather Lakes): $350,000 to $375,000 median
- Riverview 33578 (Boyette Farms, Panther Trace): $360,000 to $385,000 median

Wesley Chapel and Lutz are running above comparable Valrico 33594 on price, which surprises some buyers. The premium reflects the volume of new construction in Wesley Chapel and the buyer demand from the Tampa Bay area's north-of-the-bay migration pattern.

## Schools: The Factor That Drives the Biggest Premium

Schools are the single most significant driver of price differentiation between specific ZIP codes in both counties. And on this metric, east Hillsborough County holds a meaningful advantage.

### East Hillsborough School Zones

Valrico 33596 feeds to **Newsome High School**, consistently ranked among the top five high schools in Hillsborough County and in the top tier statewide. Homes in the Newsome attendance zone carry a $20,000 to $40,000 premium over comparable homes in the Bloomingdale High School zone. Buyers who know the data prioritize this zone, and that sustained demand floor supports prices even when the broader market softens.

Valrico 33594 feeds to **Bloomingdale High School**, which also performs well and carries a school zone premium, though less dramatic than Newsome's.

Brandon's southern sections (33511, 33596) also feed Bloomingdale High, which creates a demand corridor along the Brandon/Valrico boundary.

### Pasco County School Zones

Pasco County has strong school options, but the county does not have an equivalent to the Newsome High School premium zone in its western corridor.

Wesley Chapel High School (serving 33544 and parts of 33545) has improved significantly and earns strong community reviews. Wiregrass Ranch High School (serving parts of 33543 and 33545) has an outstanding academic reputation and is often cited as Pasco County's top-ranked high school. The schools are genuinely competitive, but the market has not priced them at a Newsome-equivalent premium.

For buyers with school-age children, the east Hillsborough zone map creates a more legible value signal. You can specifically target the Newsome zone in Valrico 33596, pay the zone premium, and have high confidence that the premium will hold at resale. Pasco's school zone premium is real but less concentrated.

## New Construction: Pasco County's Big Advantage

If new construction is your priority, Pasco County wins by a wide margin. Wesley Chapel and Land O Lakes have been among the highest-volume new construction markets in the entire Tampa Bay area for years, and builder activity remained significant through 2026.

The major builders active in Pasco County's western corridor include DR Horton, Pulte, Lennar, Maronda, and KB Home. Wesley Chapel alone added thousands of new homes across communities like Epperson, Mirada, Encanto, Westgate, and Estancia at Wiregrass.

As of late 2026, builders in Pasco County are offering meaningful incentives to move inventory, including interest rate buydowns to the 5.5 to 6.0 percent range, closing cost contributions, and free structural upgrades. These incentives are real and they meaningfully lower your first-year carrying cost.

**Epperson in Wesley Chapel** is worth specific mention. It is home to the country's first publicly available Crystal Lagoon, a 7.5-acre inland lagoon with sandy beaches and water activities. The lifestyle amenity is genuinely unique and impossible to replicate in an established east Hillsborough neighborhood. Homes here start in the $380,000 to $420,000 range for smaller floor plans and climb above $600,000 for the larger builds.

East Hillsborough County has new construction in Valrico and Riverview, but the volume is lower. Communities like Northwood Estates (Westbay Homes) in Valrico 33596 and several Riverview communities add supply, but do not match the scale of Pasco County's output.

For a first-time buyer or someone who wants to minimize maintenance risk, a new construction home in a Pasco community with strong builder incentives may deliver better total value in 2027 than a resale home in Valrico at the same price.

## Commute: The Math That Often Decides It

Commute time and cost is where east Hillsborough County consistently wins for buyers who work in Tampa, MacDill AFB, or the Selmon Expressway corridor.

**From Valrico to Downtown Tampa:** 25 to 35 minutes via I-75 during peak hours. The route is predictable and mostly highway. No toll.

**From Valrico to MacDill AFB:** 35 to 50 minutes via US-301 or SR-60 to Selmon. Manageable for military families.

**From Wesley Chapel to Downtown Tampa:** 40 to 55 minutes during peak hours via I-75. The drive is entirely highway, but the distance adds 15 to 20 minutes over Valrico.

**From Land O Lakes to Downtown Tampa:** 40 to 60 minutes via I-275 or US-41. I-275 through Tampa can be unpredictable during rush hour.

**From Wesley Chapel to South Tampa employment (MacDill area):** 50 to 70 minutes during peak hours. This commute, repeated daily, is a meaningful quality-of-life drag.

For buyers who work in Pasco County (Tampa Premium Outlets area, BayCare, AdventHealth Wesley Chapel) or at the growing corporate campuses along SR-54, the commute math flips. Wesley Chapel and Land O Lakes are genuinely convenient for north-of-Tampa employment, and commuting south to Tampa is offset by working locally.

**The I-75 toll calculation also matters.** Buyers driving from Wesley Chapel to central Tampa via I-75 and then the Selmon Expressway can pay $6 to $10 round-trip in tolls daily, depending on route and transponder. Over a 250-workday year, that is $1,500 to $2,500 in annual toll costs that do not exist for most Valrico-to-Tampa commutes.

## HOA Fees and CDD Assessments: Know Your Total Monthly Cost

This is where both markets have traps for unprepared buyers.

Pasco County's newer master-planned communities almost universally carry Community Development Districts (CDDs). A CDD is a special taxing district that finances community infrastructure -- roads, drainage, parks, amenities. The assessment is added to your property tax bill and typically runs $1,500 to $3,500 per year ($125 to $290 per month on top of base property taxes). Plus HOA fees of $100 to $200 per month.

A Wesley Chapel home priced at $420,000 may carry $500 to $600 per month in HOA and CDD costs before you get to your mortgage payment.

East Hillsborough County's established neighborhoods -- Bloomingdale, Buckhorn, most of Valrico -- were built in the 1990s and 2000s before CDDs were common. Most Valrico communities either have no HOA, or HOAs with fees in the $50 to $150 per month range, with no CDD. The monthly savings over a comparable Pasco community can run $300 to $400 per month, which translates to $3,600 to $4,800 per year.

For a buyer underwriting at the same monthly payment ceiling, the absence of a CDD in Valrico means they can afford a higher mortgage payment -- and thus a higher purchase price or lower rate -- compared to a Pasco community with identical sticker price but $250 per month in CDD fees.

## Lifestyle Considerations: Which Market Fits Your Life?

**Choose Pasco County if:**
- New construction with warranty protection is a priority and you want to avoid the deferred maintenance risk of a 20- to 30-year-old home
- The Crystal Lagoon lifestyle at Epperson or Mirada appeals to your family
- You work in Pasco County's growing employment corridor (SR-54, US-41 near Land O Lakes, BayCare/AdventHealth campus)
- You are willing to trade commute time for lower entry prices and builder incentives
- Large lot size is less important and you want community amenities

**Choose East Hillsborough County if:**
- Newsome High School zone access is a priority (Valrico 33596)
- You want to minimize recurring HOA and CDD costs
- Your employment is in Tampa, South Tampa, MacDill AFB, or the Selmon corridor
- You prefer established neighborhoods with mature trees and neighborhood character
- Resale liquidity matters: east Hillsborough resale inventory turns faster because of the established buyer pool

For families with school-age children who can target Valrico 33596, east Hillsborough generally wins on the five-year value trajectory because the Newsome zone premium is durable and self-reinforcing. For buyers without school-age children who want new construction and community lifestyle amenities, Pasco County's value proposition is genuine.

## The Investment Property Angle

Investors looking at both markets will find different yield profiles.

**Pasco County rental yields:** A $400,000 home in Wesley Chapel rents for approximately $2,100 to $2,400 per month. Gross yield of approximately 6.3 to 7.2 percent. Net cap rate after expenses, HOA, and CDD typically runs 3.8 to 4.8 percent. The CDD erodes cash flow significantly.

**East Hillsborough rental yields:** A $380,000 home in Valrico 33594 (no CDD) rents for approximately $2,100 to $2,300 per month. Gross yield of approximately 6.6 to 7.3 percent. Net cap rate runs 4.5 to 5.5 percent because of the absence of CDD costs.

For pure cash flow, Valrico and Brandon beat Wesley Chapel on a per-dollar basis, primarily because CDD fees do not apply and acquisition costs are similar or lower.

## Bottom Line: Two Markets With Different Value Propositions

Pasco County and east Hillsborough County are both legitimate choices for Tampa Bay buyers in 2027. The choice comes down to your employment location, school priorities, tolerance for commute, and preference for new construction versus established neighborhood character.

Buyers who prioritize school zone quality and commute efficiency to Tampa should focus on Valrico 33596 and the Newsome zone. The premium is real, the liquidity is strong, and the absence of CDDs makes the math work better than Pasco comparables.

Buyers who want new construction, community amenities, and are willing to drive further for work -- or work locally in Pasco County -- will find genuine value in Wesley Chapel and Land O Lakes communities, particularly with builder incentives active in late 2026 and early 2027.

Most buyers searching for a gated community in Valrico immediately think of River Hills Country Club on Lithia Pinecrest Road. River Hills is larger, more prominent, and better known. But there is a second gated community in Valrico worth knowing: Arista. It is smaller, tucked away, and often overlooked by buyers doing a broad search. For the right buyer, that obscurity is an advantage.

This guide covers everything buyers need to know about Arista in Valrico, Florida, including how it compares to River Hills, what homes trade for in today's market, the school zone reality, and what the gated lifestyle actually delivers at this price point.

## Where Arista Is Located

Arista is located in the heart of Valrico in the 33596 ZIP code, near Valrico Lake and adjacent to the Diamond Hill Golf Course. The community is accessible off Buckhorn Road in central Valrico, placing it among the established residential corridors of east Hillsborough County.

The 33596 ZIP is the higher-value of Valrico's two ZIP codes. Its median sale price runs approximately $468,996, compared to $378,907 for the 33594 ZIP. The premium exists almost entirely because of the Newsome High School zone, which covers most of 33596 including the Arista community.

## The Community

Arista is a small, gated single-family home community of approximately 60 to 70 homes. It was developed by Taylor Morrison, a publicly traded national homebuilder known for quality construction and thoughtful floor plan design. The homes in Arista were built primarily in the mid-2000s to late 2000s.

Homes in the community range from approximately 2,500 to 3,500 square feet. Floor plans offer 2 to 5 bedrooms, 3 to 4.5 bathrooms, and 2 or 3-car garages. At the upper end of the size range, some floor plans include a loft that can function as a fifth bedroom with a dedicated bath.

The architecture reflects the Taylor Morrison style of that era: well-structured exteriors with mix of stone and stucco finishes, larger footprints than surrounding entry-level communities, and interior layouts that live larger than comparable homes at the same square footage in older Valrico subdivisions.

**What Arista is not:** This is not a master-planned resort community with a clubhouse, beach entry pool, or extensive amenity package. Arista is a gated residential enclave. The value proposition is the security of a controlled-access environment, the quality of the Taylor Morrison construction, and the location within the 33596 Newsome zone.

## The Gate and What It Actually Means

Arista is a gated community with controlled access. This is meaningful in ways that buyers sometimes underestimate and sometimes overestimate.

**What gating delivers:**
- Controlled access eliminates cut-through traffic, which is one of the most common quality-of-life complaints in Valrico's open subdivision system along busy connector roads
- Delivery drivers, guests, and service providers check in at the gate rather than circulating freely
- The physical boundary creates a defined neighborhood identity that most open subdivisions cannot replicate
- Insurance carriers sometimes view gated communities favorably, though the premium impact varies by carrier

**What gating does not deliver:**
- Immunity from all crime. Determined actors bypass any gate. Gating deters casual crime and reduces certain types of incidents, but it is not a security guarantee.
- Higher appreciation than ungated communities by default. The appreciation in 33596 is driven primarily by the school zone, not the gate. River Hills and open Newsome zone communities both appreciate based on the same underlying driver.

The gate at Arista is real security infrastructure, not a decorative arch. That distinction matters for buyers who are specifically choosing a gated community for safety reasons.

## School Zone: The Newsome High School Connection

This is the most financially significant feature of an Arista address.

Arista falls within the Newsome High School zone in Hillsborough County. Newsome High School has consistently earned A ratings from both the Florida Department of Education and Niche, and it ranks among the top high schools in Hillsborough County. The school's graduation rate, SAT and ACT performance, and college acceptance data consistently place it above most other Hillsborough public high schools.

The market has priced the Newsome zone at a substantial premium: the 33596 median is approximately $90,000 above the 33594 median, and that gap has been stable for several years. It has not compressed meaningfully despite the broader market correction of 2025 and early 2026.

For families with children in middle school or early high school, an Arista purchase secures the Newsome zone assignment at the critical time. The school zone is based on your primary residence address at the time of enrollment.

Feeder schools for the Arista area include Burns Middle School (Valrico) and elementary schools in the Valrico system. Always verify your specific address on the Hillsborough County Schools boundary locator at hcps.net before making a purchase decision based on school zone assignment.

## Home Prices in Arista: What the Market Reflects

Arista is a small community, which means sales volume is inherently thin. You may see only 3 to 8 homes sell in any given 12-month period, which means each sale has outsized influence on the comparable data for the community.

In the current 2026 market, Arista homes have traded in a range from approximately $485,000 to $670,000 depending on square footage, condition, updates, and lot position within the community. Price per square foot in 33596 generally runs $210 to $230 for non-River Hills properties in good condition. Arista homes trade within that range or slightly above it when they have been well-maintained or renovated.

**What drives value within Arista:**
- Square footage and bedroom count are the primary value drivers, as they are in most communities
- Kitchen and bathroom updates matter significantly for homes in this era; dated kitchens in otherwise good condition trade at a meaningful discount to updated comps
- Lot position within the community can affect value; homes backing to community green space or with larger lots command small premiums
- Roof age: homes from this era are approaching or at the roof replacement window. A newer roof (under 10 years) is a material positive on insurance costs and buyer confidence

**What buyers should not assume:** Do not assume that every home in Arista trades at the same price per square foot. A 2,600-square-foot home with original 2007 kitchen and bathrooms will sell at a different price per square foot than a 3,200-square-foot home with a 2023 kitchen renovation. Comp analysis for Arista requires careful attention to condition and updates, not just square footage.

## How Arista Compares to River Hills

River Hills Country Club is Valrico's most prominent gated community. The comparison is natural and worth understanding:

**Scale:** River Hills is significantly larger, with multiple sections and a full 18-hole semi-private golf course. Arista is a fraction of the size, approximately 60 to 70 homes versus several hundred in River Hills. Smaller communities mean fewer neighbors but also fewer comps and thinner market liquidity.

**Price range:** River Hills spans a wide range, from entry-level homes in the $500,000 to $600,000 range up to custom estates exceeding $1 million. Arista's price range is more concentrated: approximately $485,000 to $670,000. For buyers who want a gated community but cannot stretch to River Hills custom home pricing, Arista offers a legitimate alternative.

**Golf:** River Hills includes an 18-hole semi-private golf course within the community. Arista is adjacent to Diamond Hill Golf Course but does not include golf as an integrated community feature. Residents who want to play Diamond Hill do so separately, as any public or semi-private course golfer would.

**HOA structure:** Both communities have HOA fees. River Hills has a more complex fee structure given the scale of the community and golf infrastructure. Arista's HOA fees reflect a smaller community's cost structure. Verify the current HOA fee with a listing agent on any specific Arista property before making an offer, as fees can change and vary by section.

**School zone:** Both communities fall within the 33596 Newsome High School zone. This is the same underlying value driver for both.

**Which is right for you:** River Hills makes more sense if golf access, larger custom homes, or a more established community identity matter. Arista makes more sense if you want a gated address in the Newsome zone at a lower price point, a smaller community feel, or Taylor Morrison construction quality with modern floor plans.

## What Buying in Arista in 2026 Actually Looks Like

The 2026 market across Valrico and east Hillsborough County has meaningfully more buyer leverage than the 2021 to 2022 period. Arista is no exception.

**Days on market:** Homes in 33596 are averaging 57 to 73 days on market in late 2026 summer and fall. Arista, as a niche submarket, can see longer or shorter individual marketing times depending on how well a specific home is priced relative to the current comps. Overpriced Arista listings sit. Correctly priced ones move.

**Negotiating room:** Seller-paid closing cost contributions of $8,000 to $15,000 are achievable in the $500,000 to $650,000 price range in Valrico when a home has been on the market 45 or more days. Use these contributions to buy down your mortgage rate. At 6.66 percent (Freddie Mac PMMS, week of August 28, 2026), a 0.5 point rate buydown funded by seller concessions saves approximately $1,200 annually on a $480,000 loan.

**Inspection considerations for homes of this era:** Taylor Morrison homes from the 2006 to 2008 vintage are solidly built, but every home approaching 15 to 18 years of age warrants a thorough inspection. Key items to evaluate: roof condition and age (critical for homeowners insurance pricing in Florida's current market), HVAC system condition, plumbing fixtures, and any deferred maintenance.

**Insurance:** Homeowners insurance in Hillsborough County has increased significantly since 2022. A home in the $520,000 to $600,000 replacement cost range will typically cost $3,200 to $5,000 per year for a standard HO-3 policy, depending on roof age, construction type, and carrier. A roof over 15 years old may trigger re-roofing requirements or dramatically higher premiums from some carriers. Factor this into your due diligence.

## The Right Buyer for Arista

Arista is a specific fit, not a universal fit. The buyer who gets the most value from an Arista purchase typically looks like this:

- Families with children entering middle school or high school who need a confirmed Newsome zone address
- Buyers who value the security and quality-of-life benefits of gated access in a residential neighborhood
- Move-up buyers from 33594 who want to upgrade both their home and their school zone in one transaction
- Buyers who find River Hills pricing above their budget but want a gated 33596 community
- Out-of-state relocating families prioritizing school zone reputation above all else, who want a community that feels defined and controlled rather than open

Arista is not the right fit for buyers who need extensive amenity infrastructure (pool, gym, social spaces), buyers prioritizing maximum square footage at the lowest price per square foot, or buyers who want a large selection of current listings to choose from. In a 60 to 70 home community, available inventory at any moment is typically 1 to 4 properties. You need to be ready to move when the right home comes available.

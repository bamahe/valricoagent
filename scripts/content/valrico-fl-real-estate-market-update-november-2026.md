## Valrico FL Home Prices: November 2026 by ZIP Code

Valrico sits across two ZIP codes with meaningfully different price levels. Understanding which ZIP you are shopping in -- and why the gap exists -- is foundational to making an accurate offer.

### 33594: Bloomingdale, Twin Lakes, Copper Ridge

The 33594 ZIP covers the western and southern portions of Valrico, including the Bloomingdale corridor off SR-60, Twin Lakes, Copper Ridge, and several smaller subdivisions. As of early November 2026:

- **Median sale price:** approximately $372,000 to $382,000
- **Median list price on active inventory:** approximately $395,000 to $415,000
- **Price reduction rate:** 37 to 42 percent of active listings
- **Median days on market:** 50 to 68 days
- **Active listings:** approximately 135 to 150 homes

Prices in 33594 are essentially flat to down 1 to 2 percent year over year. The softening is concentrated in the $380,000 to $450,000 tier, where rate-constrained move-up buyers have largely exited. Entry-level inventory under $360,000 continues to turn faster because it qualifies for larger buyer pools.

### 33596: Buckhorn, River Hills, Diamond Hill, Newsome Zone

The 33596 ZIP covers east Valrico, including the Buckhorn, River Hills, Diamond Hill, and Buckhorn Preserve communities. The Newsome High School attendance zone gives this ZIP a persistent demand floor that 33594 does not have. As of early November 2026:

- **Median sale price:** approximately $462,000 to $472,000
- **Median list price on active inventory:** approximately $490,000 to $515,000
- **Price reduction rate:** 33 to 38 percent of active listings
- **Median days on market:** 42 to 58 days
- **Active listings:** approximately 95 to 115 homes

The $10,000 to $15,000 decline from the early-October 33596 median reflects a mix of longer-carrying listings finally taking reductions, not a broad decline in underlying demand. Well-priced, move-in-ready homes in the Newsome zone continue to go under contract in 21 to 35 days.

### Blended Valrico Median

Across both ZIPs, the blended median sale price in November 2026 is approximately $413,000 to $422,000. That is essentially flat year over year and represents a pause following the 2020-2022 appreciation cycle.

## Inventory and Supply Conditions

The total number of active listings in Valrico reached approximately 246 homes in late October 2026, up from roughly 172 in August. That 43 percent inventory expansion over a three-month window is the clearest signal of the shift toward buyers.

At current absorption rates, supply is running at approximately 5.8 to 6.4 months. Six months is the traditional threshold between a balanced market and a buyer's market. Valrico crossed that line in late summer and has not pulled back.

For context, the Tampa Bay metro-wide median sale-to-list ratio sits at approximately 0.97 to 0.98, meaning homes are closing 2 to 3 percent below asking. In Valrico's higher-priced tier, well-negotiated buyers are achieving 3 to 5 percent below original list price, particularly on homes that have been listed 60 days or more.

**What this means for buyers:** You are no longer competing against 8 to 12 offers. In the current market, a well-researched offer with standard contingencies -- inspection, financing, appraisal -- will be considered seriously and negotiated, not rejected outright. Your goal should be aligning your offer with comparable sold data, not with the seller's list price.

**What this means for sellers:** Homes that are priced to October and November sold comps, not to spring peak-season expectations, are still selling. Homes that are priced to spring expectations are sitting and accumulating days on market, which further erodes negotiating position. If your list price was set more than 30 days ago and you have not had a showing in two weeks, the price needs to come down.

## Mortgage Rate Impact on November Affordability

The Federal Reserve's rate decisions in 2026 have not produced the mortgage rate relief that was widely anticipated at the start of the year. Thirty-year fixed mortgage rates for well-qualified borrowers (20 percent down, 740-plus credit score) are running approximately 6.80 to 7.40 percent as of November 2026, depending on lender and loan structure. See [Bankrate's current mortgage rate tracker](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/mortgage-rates-forecast/) for the latest figures.

For a Valrico buyer at the blended median of $418,000 with 10 percent down:

- **Loan amount:** $376,200
- **Rate at 7.10%:** monthly P&I of approximately $2,526
- **Property taxes (Hillsborough County):** approximately $360 to $420/month for a home assessed near $418,000
- **Homeowner's insurance:** approximately $250 to $350/month (wind/flood risk dependent)
- **HOA (if applicable):** $35 to $125/month

**Total estimated monthly cost:** $3,200 to $3,500 for a mid-range Valrico home with an HOA. That is a $550 to $700 increase over the same home at the 6.00 percent rates seen briefly in late 2024.

The rate pressure is real, but buyers who qualify at today's rates are entering a market where seller concessions -- rate buydowns, closing cost assistance, and price reductions -- are available in ways they were not in 2021 or 2022. Buyers who ask for a 1-point rate buydown from the seller, for example, can reduce their effective rate for the first one to two years of the loan, partially offsetting current market rates.

## Price Tier Breakdown: Where Buyers Are Finding Value in November 2026

### Under $360,000 in 33594

Homes in this tier qualify for larger pools of first-time buyers. Inventory here is thinner and moves faster. Expect to act within five to ten days of a new listing at a price point that pencils out. This tier typically includes smaller homes (1,400 to 1,800 square feet), older construction (1985 to 1998), or homes with functional updates needed.

### $360,000 to $450,000 in 33594

This is the softest tier in Valrico right now. Rate-constrained move-up buyers who would normally occupy this segment have slowed their purchasing. Sellers here are carrying the most price reduction pressure. Patient buyers with conventional financing can negotiate meaningfully, particularly on homes listed 45 days or more.

### $450,000 to $550,000 in 33596

The Newsome-zone premium keeps this tier more competitive than its price level might suggest in another suburb. Buyers shopping this range should expect 28 to 45 day average marketing times on well-maintained homes and less room to negotiate below 97 percent of list. Fixer-uppers in this tier are where the real negotiating leverage exists.

### $550,000 and Above in 33596

River Hills, Diamond Hill, and the upper Buckhorn Preserve segment. Days on market run longer at 55 to 85 days. Sellers in this tier have the most flexibility to negotiate, and concessions including closing cost contributions and rate buydowns are more common. Buyers in this range should not hesitate to ask for 3 to 5 percent concessions.

## Q4 Seasonal Dynamics: What Changes in November and December

November and December bring a structural shift in Valrico's buyer-seller dynamics. Here is what the data consistently shows:

**Buyer competition drops sharply.** The buyers who were shopping in March, April, and May have largely either bought or paused. November sees roughly 30 to 40 percent fewer competing buyer transactions. For the buyers still active, that means less pressure, more time to inspect, and more room to negotiate.

**Sellers are more motivated.** Sellers who have been on the market since August or September are now carrying four to five months of holding costs, seasonal pressure, and holiday stress. Many will negotiate on price, timing, or concessions to close before January.

**Holiday market creates a January reset.** The market effectively pauses from approximately December 20 through January 5. Sellers who need to close before year-end must have a contract in place by late November. Buyers who want to maximize negotiating leverage have a window from now through approximately November 20 before the holiday freeze.

**New listings dip.** Fewer sellers enter the market in November and December than at any other time of year. This means active inventory may shrink slightly through mid-December, which is slightly better for sellers who price correctly and slightly reduces buyer choice on new options.

## What Buyers Should Do Right Now

The buyer opportunity in Valrico in November 2026 is real but time-limited. Here is the action plan:

1. **Get pre-approved now, not when you find a home.** With rates at 6.80 to 7.40 percent, know exactly what you qualify for before you start making offers. Pre-approval also signals seriousness to motivated sellers.
2. **Focus on homes listed 45 days or longer.** These sellers have received market feedback. They are more likely to negotiate on price and concessions.
3. **Ask for seller concessions.** A rate buydown of 1 to 2 points, or closing cost contributions of 2 to 3 percent, are reasonable asks in this market. They directly lower your effective cost of ownership.
4. **Don't skip inspection contingencies.** With Florida's insurance market, a professional home inspection and wind mitigation report are essential before committing. Sellers in today's market accept these terms.
5. **Target the Newsome zone if schools matter.** The 33596 ZIP with Newsome High School attendance has demonstrated better price stability through softer markets. For buyers with school-age children, the premium is worth examining closely.

## What Sellers Should Do Right Now

If you need to sell before year-end, the window to list and close is narrow.

1. **Price to November comps, not spring comps.** Pull the last 60 days of closed sales in your specific subdivision and price tier. Spring 2026 sales are irrelevant. What closed in September and October tells you where the buyer is.
2. **Offer a buyer concession upfront.** A $7,500 to $12,000 closing cost or rate buydown credit included in your original listing will attract more buyers than waiting to negotiate reactively.
3. **Be on the market by November 10 to 15.** Buyers who close by December 31 need to be under contract by late November. If your home is not listed and showing by mid-November, you are targeting a January transaction, not a December one.
4. **Consider condition aggressively.** Buyers today use home inspection results as a negotiation tool. Address the big-ticket mechanical and roof items before listing, or price to reflect deferred maintenance.

## The Bigger Picture: Tampa Bay and the 2027 Forecast

Valrico does not operate in isolation. The Tampa Bay metro area is described by analysts at [Homebuyinginstitute.com](https://homebuyinginstitute.com/mortgage/tampa-buyers-market-into-2027/) as likely to remain a buyer's market well into 2027, driven by elevated rates, insurance costs pushing up carrying costs, and a return of pre-pandemic supply levels.

For Valrico specifically, the east Hillsborough corridor has structural demand support that many other Tampa Bay suburbs lack: top-performing schools in 33596, proximity to the SR-60 and I-75 employment corridors, and a suburban lifestyle package (larger lots, lower density) that continues to attract Tampa-area families. These factors will not produce a sharp appreciation recovery in 2027, but they are protective of prices in the face of a regional softening.

The most likely scenario for Valrico in 2027 is continued flat-to-modest price appreciation of 1 to 3 percent, driven by steady migration from higher-cost metros rather than speculative demand. Buyers who purchase in the fourth quarter of 2026 are entering at a moment of maximum negotiating leverage and locking in before any potential rate relief tightens the market.

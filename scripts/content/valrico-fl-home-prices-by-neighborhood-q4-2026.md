## Understanding Valrico's Two ZIP Codes

Valrico's two ZIP codes run on meaningfully different market dynamics and should be treated as separate markets for pricing purposes.

**ZIP Code 33594** covers the western and southern sections of Valrico, including Bloomingdale, Twin Lakes, Copper Ridge, Canterbury Club, Wellington, Meadowgrove, and several smaller subdivisions along Bloomingdale Avenue and Lumsden Road. The 33594 market in Q4 2026 is running approximately $370,000 to $395,000 blended median, with a price-per-square-foot of $185 to $205 depending on condition and lot size. Days on market average 42 to 58 days across the ZIP.

**ZIP Code 33596** covers the more established, higher-priced communities of eastern Valrico, including Buckhorn, Buckhorn Preserve, River Hills Country Club, Diamond Hill, Copper Ridge East, and Valrico Forest. The 33596 market in Q4 2026 sits at approximately $460,000 to $478,000 blended median. Price-per-square-foot runs $215 to $245 for standard homes and climbs above $300 for golf course and estate properties. The Newsome High School attendance zone, which includes most of 33596, creates a persistent demand premium that has insulated this ZIP from the broader Hillsborough County softening.

## Bloomingdale: Most Established, Best Value in 33594

**Q4 2026 price range:** $325,000 to $535,000
**Median sale price, trailing 90 days:** approximately $382,000 to $395,000
**Price per square foot:** $182 to $210
**Days on market:** 38 to 62 days
**HOA:** Varies by section. Many original Bloomingdale sections have no HOA or nominal annual fees under $300. Newer pockets carry fees of $400 to $600 per year. No section carries a CDD.
**School zone:** Bloomingdale High School (east Hillsborough)

Bloomingdale is the most heavily traded neighborhood in 33594 and represents the backbone of Valrico's entry-to-mid market. Homes were built primarily from the late 1980s through the early 2000s, which means mature tree canopy, larger lot sizes (most homes sit on 6,000 to 10,000 square feet, some on quarter-acre to half-acre lots), and established landscaping that newer communities cannot replicate.

The price range in Bloomingdale is wider than any other Valrico neighborhood. At the entry level ($325,000 to $365,000), buyers are getting 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom homes from 1,500 to 1,800 square feet in need of cosmetic updates. At the midrange ($380,000 to $450,000), 4-bedroom homes with 2,100 to 2,600 square feet in updated condition. The upper tier of Bloomingdale ($475,000 to $535,000) includes estate homes on larger lots with pools, three-car garages, and substantial interior updates.

**Who buys in Bloomingdale:** First-time buyers, families relocating from higher-cost markets, buyers who prioritize lot size over newness, and investors buying 1990s-era homes with upside renovation potential.

**The current leverage window:** Homes at 45 to 65 days on market in Bloomingdale are where Q4 buyers are finding the best terms. Sellers who have already taken one price reduction and are still not under contract by mid-November are the most negotiable group. Closing cost credits of $6,000 to $10,000 are common on this inventory. Rate buydowns are available on homes above $400,000 where the carrying cost has limited the buyer pool.

## Buckhorn: Largest Community, Steadiest Demand

**Q4 2026 price range:** $380,000 to $495,000
**Median sale price, trailing 90 days:** approximately $428,000 to $445,000
**Price per square foot:** $200 to $230
**Days on market:** 28 to 48 days
**HOA:** Nominal annual fees, typically $200 to $600. No CDD.
**School zone:** Newsome High School (33596) and Bloomingdale High School (sections along the northern edge)

Buckhorn is Valrico's most traded 33596 neighborhood and the clearest measure of steady demand in the east Hillsborough market. The community encompasses several thousand homes built from the early 1990s through the mid-2000s, with a mix of one-story and two-story single-family homes. Lots run larger than most comparable-priced communities in the Tampa area, with standard lots of 7,500 to 12,000 square feet and some backing to conservation areas.

The 28 to 48 day average days on market in Buckhorn Q4 2026 reflects one of the stronger absorption rates in Valrico, particularly for the Newsome zone sections. Well-priced, move-in-ready homes in Buckhorn in the $410,000 to $455,000 range are going under contract in 18 to 32 days. Homes above $460,000 are taking 50 to 70 days unless they have recent kitchen updates, a pool, or a larger-than-standard lot.

**Who buys in Buckhorn:** Move-up buyers from 33594, families prioritizing Newsome High School zoning, buyers who want established neighborhood infrastructure with relatively low HOA costs, and relocating buyers who want a proven community with a known price floor.

**Key pricing driver:** Buckhorn's northern sections fall in the Bloomingdale High School zone and trade at a consistent discount of $20,000 to $35,000 relative to comparable Buckhorn homes in the Newsome zone. Buyers who do not have children in school, or whose children are in elementary school and years away from high school, can capture that discount by focusing on the northern section.

## River Hills Country Club: Valrico's Prestige Address

**Q4 2026 price range:** $455,000 to $1,200,000+
**Median sale price, trailing 90 days:** approximately $530,000 to $575,000
**Price per square foot:** $220 to $340 for standard residences, higher for golf course estate homes
**Days on market:** 42 to 90 days on standard homes; 90 to 180 days on estate and golf course properties
**HOA:** Mandatory. River Hills Country Club has HOA fees in the range of $700 to $1,200 per year for the community association, plus optional country club membership fees separate from the HOA.
**School zone:** Newsome High School

River Hills is a gated golf course community in the southeastern corner of Valrico's 33596 ZIP code. The community was developed around the River Hills Golf and Country Club course, and many of the most distinctive homes back directly to golf fairways or sit on the community's larger estate lots. River Hills attracts a specific buyer: someone who wants the country club lifestyle, security gate, and established prestige address at a price point well below what equivalent golf community living costs in Wesley Chapel or Fishhawk Ranch.

Pricing in River Hills is bimodal. The standard interior homes (not on the golf course, not on larger-than-standard lots) trade in the $455,000 to $550,000 range and represent the community's highest-volume transaction price band. Golf course homes, especially larger estate homes with significant square footage and updated interiors, trade from $600,000 to well above $1,000,000. The upper-tier inventory has extended days on market because the buyer pool at those price points is smaller.

**Q4 2026 market condition:** River Hills has experienced more price softening in its upper tier than the standard interior tier. Golf course estate homes listed above $750,000 have averaged 100 to 160 days on market in Q4. Sellers in this range who bought pre-2020 at substantially lower prices still have equity margin to negotiate, and Q4 2026 buyers at the high end of River Hills are finding the best leverage they have had in several years.

**Who buys in River Hills:** Empty nesters, professionals relocating from higher-cost metros, buyers who want gated living and community social infrastructure, buyers with children targeting Newsome High School, and second-home buyers who want east Tampa Bay access.

## Diamond Hill: Gated Value in the Upper 33596 Tier

**Q4 2026 price range:** $450,000 to $640,000
**Median sale price, trailing 90 days:** approximately $495,000 to $525,000
**Price per square foot:** $210 to $260
**Days on market:** 35 to 65 days
**HOA:** Community has an HOA with annual fees typically in the $1,000 to $1,400 per year range. Gated entry. No CDD.
**School zone:** Newsome High School

Diamond Hill is Valrico's other gated community, sitting northeast of River Hills in the 33596 ZIP. It offers gated access and larger-than-average lot sizes at prices typically $40,000 to $60,000 below comparable-square-footage River Hills homes. For buyers who want the gated community experience but do not require a golf course or club infrastructure, Diamond Hill frequently represents better value per square foot.

Homes in Diamond Hill were built primarily from the late 1990s through the early 2000s. Standard floor plans run 2,400 to 3,600 square feet on lots of 10,000 to over 20,000 square feet. The upper end of Diamond Hill features custom estate homes on large lots that rival anything in River Hills at a lower price point.

**Current pricing dynamics:** The $475,000 to $525,000 range in Diamond Hill has the strongest Q4 2026 demand. These are the 2,500 to 3,000 square foot, 4-bedroom homes in good condition with standard pools and garages. At this range, days on market average 30 to 45 days. Above $550,000, the market slows to 60 to 90 days as buyers compare Diamond Hill to River Hills golf course homes and sometimes choose the country club address at similar pricing.

## Buckhorn Preserve: Newer Homes, Conservation Setting

**Q4 2026 price range:** $395,000 to $545,000
**Median sale price, trailing 90 days:** approximately $445,000 to $465,000
**Price per square foot:** $205 to $240
**Days on market:** 30 to 55 days
**HOA:** Moderate, typically $400 to $800 per year. No CDD.
**School zone:** Newsome High School

Buckhorn Preserve is a 33596 community with a more contemporary build vintage than much of Valrico. Homes here were built primarily from 2000 to 2015, giving buyers access to larger garages, more open floor plans, and updated mechanical systems relative to the older Bloomingdale and original Buckhorn stock. Many lots back to conservation areas, which is a meaningful selling point in a community like this.

The relative newness of Buckhorn Preserve compared to surrounding communities does not translate into higher pricing versus Buckhorn standard. Buyers pay a slight premium for the conservation backing and contemporary plans, but the community sits in a price range that overlaps substantially with Buckhorn and trades competitively with it.

**Who buys in Buckhorn Preserve:** Buyers who want newer construction without the builder warranty complexity, families who want conservation backing for privacy and wildlife appeal, and buyers who want Newsome zone access at a price point below Diamond Hill.

## Copper Ridge, Wellington, and Entry-Level 33594

**Q4 2026 price range:** $285,000 to $395,000
**Median sale price in this segment:** approximately $340,000 to $360,000
**Price per square foot:** $170 to $195
**Days on market:** 45 to 75 days
**HOA:** Varies. Many sections have no HOA. Some sections have minimal fees under $300 per year.
**School zone:** Bloomingdale High School

The entry-level sections of Valrico's 33594 ZIP code, including Copper Ridge, Wellington, Canterbury Club, Meadowgrove, and Twin Lakes, represent the most affordable segment of the east Hillsborough owner-occupied market. These neighborhoods typically feature 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom homes from 1,300 to 1,900 square feet, built in the 1990s through early 2000s.

Days on market in this segment run longer than the 33596 Newsome zone, reflecting the broader challenge of affordability-constrained buyer demand at higher mortgage rates. At 6.75% on a $310,000 loan (10% down on a $345,000 purchase), the monthly payment for principal and interest is approximately $2,010. Total housing cost with taxes, insurance, and modest HOA runs approximately $2,750 to $3,000 per month, which remains within range for dual-income households targeting this segment.

**Q4 2026 leverage:** This segment has the most inventory relative to demand in Valrico. Sellers who listed in spring and summer 2026 and are still on the market are facing real carrying costs. Closing cost credits of $5,000 to $8,000 are achievable. Rate buydowns are available from motivated sellers. Buyers who are patient, pre-approved, and focused on homes in the 50- to 80-day on-market window will find meaningful concessions in this price range.

## Q4 2026 Buyer Takeaways by Budget

**Budget $300,000 to $370,000:** Focus on Copper Ridge, Wellington, Twin Lakes, and southern Bloomingdale. Expect 1,400 to 1,900 square feet, 1990s construction, and 3 bedrooms. Leverage is highest in this range. Plan for 15 to 25 days of negotiation time and push for closing cost credits.

**Budget $370,000 to $440,000:** This is the core Bloomingdale range and the entry of northern Buckhorn. Best value for buyers who want established neighborhoods without HOA costs and are comfortable with Bloomingdale High School zone. Active inventory is healthy. Homes in good condition are still moving in 30 to 45 days.

**Budget $440,000 to $500,000:** This is the Buckhorn and Buckhorn Preserve sweet spot. Best transaction volume in 33596. If you are targeting Newsome zone at this price, Buckhorn offers the best selection. Homes at the top of this range with pools are still selling, but require 40 to 60 days of patience.

**Budget $500,000 to $600,000:** Diamond Hill and upper-tier Buckhorn. Gated access available in Diamond Hill. This range is slower in Q4 2026 than it was in spring, but motivated sellers are present. Inspection objection repairs and buydowns are negotiable.

**Budget $600,000+:** River Hills golf course and estate tier. This is Valrico's most negotiable Q4 segment. Extended days on market, motivated sellers with significant equity, and a relatively thin buyer pool create meaningful opportunity for buyers with patience. Expect 60 to 90 day negotiation timelines.

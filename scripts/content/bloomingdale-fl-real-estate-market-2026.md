Bloomingdale is Hillsborough County's largest neighborhood, with more than 5,000 homes spread across 32 subdivisions built by five developers starting in 1979. It runs along Bloomingdale Avenue between US-301 and Lithia Pinecrest Road, deep in east Hillsborough County, with a Valrico FL 33596 mailing address and a community identity strong enough that buyers search for it by name. If you are evaluating a purchase or sale here, the 2026 data tells a specific story.

## What Bloomingdale FL Actually Is

Bloomingdale is not an incorporated city. It is a census-designated place and master-planned community in unincorporated Hillsborough County. Most homes carry a Valrico FL 33596 address; a minority on the western edge carry a Brandon or Riverview mailing address despite falling squarely within Bloomingdale's boundaries.

The community shares the 33596 ZIP code with Buckhorn, Diamond Hill, River Hills Country Club, and Buckhorn Preserve. These are all distinct neighborhoods within the same ZIP. When you search for "Bloomingdale FL homes for sale," MLS results typically include all of 33596. Understanding which sub-neighborhood you are actually shopping is critical because prices, HOA structures, and school zones vary meaningfully within a half-mile of each other.

## The 32 Subdivisions: Price Ranges by Neighborhood Tier

Bloomingdale's development history produced dramatically different housing types within a few miles of each other. Here is how the major tiers break down.

### Core Bloomingdale (1979 to 1995)

The original Bloomingdale sections adjacent to Bloomingdale Avenue feature Florida ranch-style and two-story homes on lots of 0.18 to 0.30 acres. Most homes are 1,400 to 2,400 square feet. Prices run $340,000 to $460,000. HOA fees are minimal or absent in the older sections. This is the best value-per-square-foot tier in the community for buyers who can tolerate homes that need updates.

### Bloomingdale Golfers Club Corridor

Homes surrounding the Bloomingdale Golfers Club (a public 18-hole course on Natures Way Drive) carry a $20,000 to $50,000 premium over comparable non-golf-front homes. Golf-front lots in this price range are genuinely scarce. Homes built from the early 1990s through early 2000s in this section trade from $420,000 to $600,000.

### Buckhorn and Original Buckhorn Estate

Buckhorn and its premium section Original Buckhorn Estate sit southeast of core Bloomingdale, with homes built from the late 1980s through the mid-2000s. Original Buckhorn Estate recorded a $450,000 median at $222 per square foot in August 2026. Lots run 0.20 to 0.35 acres. Most homes include pools. Buckhorn feeds Buckhorn Elementary, a consistently well-rated Hillsborough County school.

### Diamond Hill

Diamond Hill is the premier non-gated neighborhood within 33596, with custom-built homes on half-acre to one-acre lots. Construction runs from the 2000s through the mid-2010s. Home sizes: 2,800 to 5,000 square feet. Price range: $550,000 to $850,000 depending on lot position and finish level. Diamond Hill sits within the Newsome High School zone, which drives a $60,000 to $100,000 premium over comparable homes in Bloomingdale HS zone properties.

### River Hills Country Club

River Hills is the only gated community in 33596, with approximately 1,162 homes around an 18-hole semi-private golf course. The Masters section median list price is $522,000 at $228 per square foot. Custom estate positions exceed $1 million. River Hills anchors the upper tier of the ZIP and is covered in detail in the [Bloomingdale and River Hills seller guide](/blog/selling-in-river-hills-or-bloomingdale-neighborhood-specific-advice/).

## Schools: The Variable That Drives Pricing

School zone is the most consequential decision in Bloomingdale real estate. Two homes a quarter-mile apart can be in different high school zones that carry a $60,000 to $100,000 price differential.

**Bloomingdale High School zone:** Most of core Bloomingdale and Buckhorn feeds to Bloomingdale High School. BHS holds an A grade on Niche, a graduation rate above 88%, and ranks in Florida's top 100 public high schools. It is the school zone anchor for the majority of properties in 33596 west of Lithia Pinecrest Road.

**Newsome High School zone:** Diamond Hill, eastern Bloomingdale sections, and portions near Lithia Pinecrest Road fall within Newsome High School's boundaries. Newsome is ranked in the top 10 to 15 public high schools in Florida. That ranking creates structural buyer demand that sustains prices in a market where everything else is softening. The [Newsome High School zone effect on Valrico home values](/blog/how-newsome-high-school-zone-impacts-valrico-home-values/) is documented in a separate post with the data behind the premium.

**Verification requirement:** Do not rely on listing descriptions for school zone accuracy. Use the Hillsborough County Schools boundary locator at [hcps.net](https://www.hcps.net) and enter the exact property address before making any offer contingent on school zone access.

**Middle and elementary schools:** Burns Middle School and Mulrennan Middle School serve the Bloomingdale area. Alafia Elementary, Cimino Elementary, Buckhorn Elementary, and Lithia Springs Elementary serve different sections. All hold positive Niche and GreatSchools ratings relative to Hillsborough County averages.

## Community Amenities

**Bloomingdale Golfers Club:** An 18-hole public golf course on Natures Way Drive. No membership required, pay-as-you-play, with published green fees on the course website. The course provides open-space buffering and a visual amenity for adjacent homes.

**Campo YMCA at Bloomingdale:** Full-service facility on Bloomingdale Avenue with indoor and outdoor pools, fitness equipment, and youth programs. Family memberships run approximately $80 to $100 per month.

**Bell Creek Trail and Preserve:** A 2.5-mile paved multi-use trail connecting Bloomingdale to the Bell Creek Preserve trail system, used heavily by cyclists, runners, and dog walkers.

**Hillsborough County Parks:** Two county parks and recreation facilities within the Bloomingdale boundary, with athletic fields, playgrounds, and passive recreation areas.

**No CDD in most sections:** The majority of Bloomingdale's 32 subdivisions predate Community Development District fee structures. Buyers save $1,500 to $3,000 per year compared to FishHawk Ranch in Lithia, Triple Creek in Riverview, or most Wesley Chapel communities. Over a 10-year hold, that gap reaches $15,000 to $30,000 in avoided carrying costs.

## What Buyers Need to Know in 2026

**Lead with school zone verification.** Two homes on the same Bloomingdale block can feed different high schools. Pull the Hillsborough County school zone map before touring, not after. The Newsome zone premium is real, but so is the value in correctly understanding that a Bloomingdale HS zone home at $390,000 may deliver better per-square-foot value for a family that does not have a Newsome HS priority.

**Pool homes command a clear premium.** A pool home in Buckhorn or core Bloomingdale sells $15,000 to $40,000 above a comparable non-pool home in the same condition. If you plan to add a pool post-closing, verify the lot geometry and HOA rules before writing an offer.

**Roof age controls your insurance cost.** Original 1990s roofs are common in the older Bloomingdale sections. Florida insurance carriers are offering limited options or elevated premiums on roofs over 15 years old. A required roof replacement runs $18,000 to $28,000. Factor this into your negotiating position and inspection budget. According to the Florida Department of Financial Services, roof age and construction type are the primary determinants of homeowner's insurance availability and pricing in the current market.

**Fall 2026 is a genuine buying window.** Homes that listed in spring 2026 and have not sold have been sitting for four to six months. Sellers at 60 or more days on market are far more open to closing cost credits and price adjustments than in the spring. The 2.5-month supply in 33596 means good homes still generate competition, but the urgency sellers felt in March is gone.

**Financing tiers:** Homes through $832,750 (the 2026 FHFA conforming loan limit) qualify for conventional financing at approximately 6.65% as of September 2026 per Freddie Mac. Diamond Hill purchases above that threshold require jumbo loans at approximately 6.76% to 6.78%. On a $700,000 purchase with 20% down, that is a $560,000 loan at roughly $3,651 per month in principal and interest. According to [Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms), rates are expected to decline modestly through Q1 2027.

## What Sellers Need to Know in 2026

**Price to the sub-neighborhood.** The 32 subdivisions produce a wide range of values within a half-mile. A 1985 Bloomingdale ranch and a 2008 Diamond Hill custom home are not comparable properties and should not be priced from the same data set. A local REMAX Collective agent with 23+ years of experience in east Hillsborough County prices to the specific sub-neighborhood, not the ZIP code average.

**Presentation separates the 24-day sales from the 71-day sales.** The dual market in 33596 is real. Professionally staged, freshly painted, move-in-ready homes are selling in 20 to 30 days. Homes with deferred maintenance or dated interiors are sitting at 60 to 90 days before price reductions begin. The investment in pre-listing preparation pays back in both speed and final price.

**Concessions are now part of the deal.** In fall 2026, buyers above $400,000 in Bloomingdale routinely ask for $5,000 to $12,000 in closing cost credits on homes with 45 or more days of market time. Budget for it as part of your net proceeds calculation rather than treating it as a surprise negotiating demand.

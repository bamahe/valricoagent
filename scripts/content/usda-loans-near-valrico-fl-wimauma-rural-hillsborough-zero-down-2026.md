## What Is a USDA Home Loan?

USDA rural housing loans come in two forms. Most buyers will use the Section 502 Guaranteed Loan Program, which works like a conventional or FHA mortgage in that you apply through an approved private lender. The USDA guarantees the loan -- similar to how FHA guarantees its loans -- which lets lenders offer zero down payment financing at competitive rates.

The second program, the Section 502 Direct Loan, is a government-to-borrower loan with subsidized interest rates as low as 1 percent for very low and low income households. It is administered directly by the USDA Rural Development office and targets buyers at lower income thresholds. Most buyers using USDA financing in the Tampa Bay area will use the guaranteed program rather than the direct program.

Key features of the USDA Guaranteed Loan:

- **Zero down payment required** -- the entire purchase price can be financed
- **30-year fixed rate** -- the same loan term available on conventional and FHA loans
- **Competitive interest rates** -- typically 25 to 50 basis points below a conventional 30-year fixed at similar credit scores
- **Upfront guarantee fee of 1 percent** of the loan amount, rolled into the loan
- **Annual fee of 0.35 percent** of the remaining loan balance, paid monthly (this replaces PMI)
- **No maximum loan amount** -- unlike FHA, there is no loan limit cap; the income limit governs eligibility, not the purchase price
- **Minimum credit score of 640** for most lenders (some go to 620 with compensating factors)

The 0.35 percent annual fee is significantly lower than FHA's 0.85 percent MIP and disappears when your loan-to-value ratio drops below 80 percent -- something FHA MIP does not do automatically on loans originated after 2013.

## Which Areas Near Valrico Qualify for USDA Loans?

This is the question that matters most and also the one with the most nuance. USDA eligibility is determined address by address using the official USDA Property Eligibility Map. The program is designed for rural and smaller suburban communities, which means the eligibility boundary runs through eastern Hillsborough County in ways that sometimes surprise buyers.

### Wimauma (33598): Confirmed USDA-Eligible

Wimauma is the most significant USDA-eligible community near Valrico. Located roughly 20 miles south on US-301 from Valrico's southern edge, Wimauma sits solidly within USDA's rural eligibility boundary. Multiple active listings in 33598 confirm USDA eligibility, and lenders who specialize in rural housing financing routinely close USDA loans in this ZIP code.

The Wimauma market in mid-2026 showed median sale prices of approximately $347,000 to $351,000, with per-square-foot pricing around $179. The market shifted buyer-favorable in 2025 and 2026, with values down approximately 2.6 to 5.6 percent year over year depending on the data source. That combination -- zero down, below-market purchase prices, and buyer leverage on negotiations -- makes Wimauma one of the most accessible entry points in the county.

For a buyer purchasing a $350,000 home in Wimauma with USDA financing at approximately 6.75 percent (USDA rates as of November 2026), the monthly payment works out to roughly:

- Principal and interest: $2,273 (on $353,500 including the 1 percent guarantee fee)
- Annual fee (0.35% of remaining balance): approximately $103/month in year one
- Property taxes: approximately $395/month (Hillsborough County millage, no homestead in year one)
- Homeowners insurance: approximately $240/month
- **Total estimated payment: $3,011/month**

Compare that to the same purchase with 3.5 percent FHA down ($12,250):

- Principal and interest: $2,245 (on $341,250 at the same 6.75% rate, with FHA upfront MIP added)
- FHA MIP (0.85% annual): approximately $241/month
- Property taxes: $395/month
- Insurance: $240/month
- **Total estimated payment: $3,121/month** -- about $110 more per month than USDA

The USDA loan saves over $12,000 at closing (the down payment) and reduces the monthly payment compared to FHA. For first-time buyers without significant cash reserves, that is a meaningful advantage.

### What Does NOT Qualify Near Valrico

Valrico itself (33594 and 33596), Brandon, Riverview, Apollo Beach, Sun City Center, and most of Plant City's annexed areas are outside the USDA eligible zone. Plant City's rural eligibility was reduced following the 2020 census as population growth pushed those areas out of the rural classification threshold. If you are shopping specifically in Valrico's established neighborhoods like Bloomingdale, Buckhorn, River Hills, or Diamond Hill, USDA financing is not available for those purchases. FHA or conventional are your options there.

## USDA Income Limits for Hillsborough County (2026)

USDA income limits for the guaranteed loan program were updated in July 2026 under USDA Procedure Notice 657. Income is counted household-wide -- every adult living in the home -- not just the people on the loan application.

For Hillsborough County under the FY2026 figures:

- **Households of 1 to 4 people:** Approximately $119,850 moderate income limit
- **Households of 5 to 8 people:** Approximately $158,250 moderate income limit

Some Florida-specific resources cite higher limits tied to the broader Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater metro definition. The figures above reflect county-level limits; verify your specific limit at [USDA's income and property eligibility site](https://eligibility.sc.egov.usda.gov). The income limit counts gross income from all sources for all adult household members, though USDA allows certain deductions (childcare expenses, medical expenses for elderly/disabled household members, dependents under 18) that can lower your qualifying household income.

A family of four earning $115,000 combined gross income would likely qualify under these limits. A single buyer earning $80,000 qualifies easily. A dual-income couple earning $125,000 combined would need to review whether deductions bring their qualifying income below the limit.

## USDA Rates in the Current Market (November 2026)

As of November 2026, USDA guaranteed loan rates from approved lenders are running approximately 25 to 50 basis points below conventional 30-year fixed rates. With conventional 30-year rates around 7.10 to 7.25 percent, USDA rates are approximately 6.65 to 6.90 percent for well-qualified borrowers with 640-plus credit scores.

That spread exists because the USDA guarantee reduces lender risk substantially, similar to the VA loan guarantee. For buyers who qualify, that rate advantage on a $350,000 loan translates to roughly $60 to $90 per month in lower principal and interest payments compared to a conventional loan at the same term.

## Property Requirements: What Homes Qualify for USDA Financing

The home itself must meet USDA's property standards. Key requirements:

- **Primary residence only** -- no investment properties or vacation homes
- **Modest size for the area** -- this is not a formal limit but very large luxury homes may face scrutiny; most standard single-family homes in Wimauma and rural Hillsborough fall well within guidelines
- **Move-in ready condition** -- the home must meet USDA minimum property standards; homes needing major structural repairs, missing appliances, or with active pest infestation issues typically fail appraisal
- **No income-producing features** -- the property must function as a residence, not a farm operation
- **Single-family detached homes or approved condominiums** -- manufactured homes on permanent foundations may qualify under specific conditions

USDA appraisals are generally similar to FHA in rigor, stricter than conventional. Sellers of homes in USDA-eligible areas who price aggressively can expand their buyer pool significantly by ensuring the home is in clean, move-in condition.

## How to Find USDA-Eligible Homes Near Valrico

Three steps for buyers:

**Step 1:** Use the USDA Property Eligibility Map at [eligibility.sc.egov.usda.gov](https://eligibility.sc.egov.usda.gov) to run any specific address before spending time on a showing. The map updates periodically following census reviews, so check each address individually.

**Step 2:** When searching on Zillow, Redfin, or the MLS, filter your search to Wimauma 33598 or Dover 33527. Many listings in USDA-eligible areas already note "USDA eligible" in the listing remarks, though listing agents do not always include this.

**Step 3:** Work with a lender who handles USDA guaranteed loans regularly. Not every lender is an approved USDA lender, and lender experience matters. USDA loans have additional processing requirements and the USDA must approve the commitment before closing, which adds time -- typically five to ten business days beyond a standard conventional closing. Budget for a 35- to 45-day close rather than 30 days.

For buyers relocating to the greater Valrico area who have flexibility on exactly which community they purchase in, Wimauma offers a compelling combination: USDA zero-down financing, prices 20 to 25 percent below Valrico's median, and a 20- to 25-minute drive to the jobs corridors along US-301, SR-60, and I-75.

Florida's November 3, 2026 election changed the financial calculus of homeownership in Valrico. Amendment 3 passed with 62 percent of the vote, clearing the 60 percent supermajority the state constitution requires. The measure is now law. The non-school homestead exemption rises from $25,000 to $150,000 effective January 1, 2027, then to $250,000 effective January 1, 2028, with annual inflation adjustments beginning in 2029.

Every 2027 Valrico market forecast written before November 3 assumed Amendment 3 passage was a variable. It is no longer a variable. It is a confirmed input. This post revises the 2027 Valrico outlook with Amendment 3 factored in as fact.

## What Amendment 3 Actually Delivers for Valrico Homeowners in 2027

The savings are ZIP-code specific because they depend on assessed value.

**Valrico 33594 (Bloomingdale, Copper Ridge, Twin Lakes):** Median assessed value near $380,000. With the non-school exemption rising from $25,000 to $150,000, taxable value for non-school purposes drops by an additional $125,000. At Hillsborough County's current non-school millage rate of approximately 7.57 mills, that saves approximately $946 per year. Monthly, that is roughly $79 off your effective carrying cost.

**Valrico 33596 (River Hills, Buckhorn, Diamond Hill, Brentwood Hills):** Median assessed value near $469,000. Same calculation produces annual savings of approximately $1,100 to $1,200. Monthly savings of $92 to $100.

**What it does not change:** The school district exemption stays at $25,000. School millage is unaffected. Your mortgage payment, HOA, and insurance are unaffected. The amendment changes the county, municipal, and special district portions of your tax bill only.

## Why This Changes the 2027 Demand Picture

Before Amendment 3 passed, the base case for 2027 Valrico demand rested on two legs: modest mortgage rate declines from the 6.5 percent range toward 5.75 to 6.25 percent, and continued Hillsborough County population growth absorbing inventory.

Amendment 3 adds a third leg. Consider what $946 to $1,200 per year means in purchasing power terms. At a 6.5 percent mortgage rate, that annualized saving is equivalent to roughly $12,000 to $15,000 of additional purchasing power for a buyer who underwrites on total monthly housing cost. Buyers who were previously priced out of the 33594 median at $380,000 may now qualify. Buyers eyeing the 33596 Newsome zone at $469,000 may move off the fence.

This is a demand accelerant that was not in pre-election models. Florida Realtors estimated Amendment 3 passage could lift home values statewide by 7 to 9 percent over time. That range is probably too optimistic for a 12-month horizon, but a near-term demand bump of 1 to 3 percent in Valrico is more grounded given current inventory levels and mortgage rate constraints.

## The December 2026 Bridge Effect

Before 2027 begins, there is a short-run demand spike happening right now. Buyers who want to own their home as of January 1, 2027 to claim the first year of Amendment 3 savings must close by December 31, 2026.

That urgency is adding motivated buyers to a market that already had seller-favorable conditions in the 33596 ZIP code (approximately 2.0 months of supply) and a more balanced 33594 (approximately 4.0 to 4.5 months of supply). Through December, expect:

- Faster movement on 33596 listings priced correctly in the $430,000 to $480,000 range
- Sellers with 33594 homes in the $350,000 to $420,000 range seeing stronger traffic than the typical November slowdown would produce
- Some buyers accepting slightly less negotiating leverage in exchange for a December 31 close

This December bridge effect is temporary. By mid-January, it fades. But it sets the starting point for the 2027 spring market at a higher level of active demand than a typical January reset would suggest.

## Revised 2027 Price Forecast: Three Scenarios

**Base Case (most likely, 60 percent probability):** Mortgage rates decline modestly from 6.5 percent to approximately 6.0 to 6.25 percent by mid-2027, consistent with Fannie Mae and Mortgage Bankers Association forecasts. Amendment 3 savings take effect January 1. Hillsborough County population growth continues at the 25,000 to 30,000 annual pace.

Valrico 33594: 3 to 5 percent appreciation over 2027. Median price moves from approximately $378,907 to $391,000 to $398,000.
Valrico 33596: 4 to 6 percent appreciation over 2027. Median price moves from approximately $468,996 to $488,000 to $497,000.

The 33596 premium persists because Newsome High School zone demand is structural, not cyclical. School zone buyers are less sensitive to mortgage rate fluctuations than general buyers because they are optimizing for a specific geographic constraint.

**Upside Case (25 percent probability):** Rates decline to 5.5 to 5.75 percent by Q2 2027, unlocking meaningful pent-up buyer demand. Amendment 3 demand bump combines with rate relief to push Valrico appreciation toward 6 to 9 percent. Inventory tightens, days on market compresses to under 30 days in 33596. Sellers regain full pricing power.

This scenario is most likely if the Federal Reserve cuts the federal funds rate two to three additional times between November 2026 and April 2027, which would pull 30-year mortgage rates toward the mid-5 percent range.

**Downside Case (15 percent probability):** Rates stay above 6.75 percent through 2027 due to persistent inflation or economic uncertainty. Amendment 3 demand boost is absorbed by continued inventory growth. Valrico appreciation is flat to 1 percent. Days on market remain in the 60 to 80 day range. Buyers retain negotiating leverage through most of 2027.

## What This Means for the Newsome Zone Specifically

The Valrico 33596 / Newsome High School zone is the most defensible submarket in this forecast because demand is derived from school zoning, not just housing preferences. Families with school-age children cannot achieve the same school zone outcome by buying in 33594, Brandon, or Riverview. That inelastic demand provides a floor that the broader Hillsborough County market does not have.

As of November 2026, 33596 median sold prices are running approximately $468,996, compared to a pre-pandemic (2019) median of roughly $280,000 to $300,000. That appreciation was driven by pandemic demand and low inventory. The question for 2027 is whether the Newsome premium can hold.

The data says yes. As long as Newsome High School maintains its performance profile (Niche grade, Newsweek and U.S. News rankings, graduation rate), families will pay the premium. Amendment 3 makes that premium more affordable in year-over-year carrying cost terms. The 33596 market in spring 2027 is likely to be the tightest segment in east Hillsborough County.

## Inventory Trajectory: What to Watch

Valrico entered November 2026 with approximately 240 to 260 active listings across both ZIP codes, representing roughly 5.8 to 6.4 months of supply in 33594 and approximately 2.0 months of supply in 33596. These are the numbers from which the 2027 market launches.

For the 33596 Newsome zone market to remain seller-favorable in spring 2027, inventory must stay below 2.5 to 3.0 months. The current trajectory suggests it will, given:

- Limited new construction in 33596 relative to 33594 (most new homes are in Riverview and Wesley Chapel)
- School zone-driven demand resetting in January as families begin the spring buying cycle
- Amendment 3 bringing additional buyers to 33596 who were previously stretched on carrying cost

For 33594, the 4.0 to 4.5 months of supply means buyers will retain leverage through at least Q1 2027. Price reductions and seller concessions will remain common. Appreciation in 33594 in 2027 depends more on mortgage rate movement than on Amendment 3 alone.

## Mortgage Rate Outlook for Valrico Buyers in 2027

The mortgage rate environment for 2027 is more favorable than 2026 but not dramatically so. Consensus forecasts from Fannie Mae, the Mortgage Bankers Association, and Freddie Mac point to:

- Late 2026: 6.3 to 6.5 percent for a 30-year fixed
- Q1 2027: 6.0 to 6.3 percent
- Mid-2027: 5.75 to 6.25 percent
- Late 2027: 5.5 to 6.0 percent (dependent on inflation data and Fed policy)

These projections assume one to two additional Federal Reserve rate cuts between now and mid-2027. If inflation data surprises to the upside, rate relief could be slower. If the economy softens more than expected, rate relief could accelerate.

At 6.0 percent on a $380,000 loan (purchasing a $475,000 home with 20 percent down), monthly principal and interest is $2,278, down from approximately $2,403 at 6.5 percent. That $125 monthly improvement adds purchasing power equivalent to roughly $20,000 to $25,000. Combined with the Amendment 3 savings of $946 to $1,200 per year, the total improvement in annual carrying cost for a 2027 buyer versus a 2026 buyer buying the same home could exceed $2,400 to $2,700 per year.

That delta is what will draw buyers off the sidelines in 2027.

## What Sellers Should Do Before the Spring 2027 Market

If you are a Valrico homeowner planning to sell in 2027, the revised outlook is favorable, but timing still matters. Here is what the Amendment 3 passage and the rate trajectory suggest for your strategy.

**List in January or February 2027.** Demand is resetting upward, but the spring market in Valrico traditionally starts showing increased buyer traffic in February. Listing in January positions you ahead of the competing listings that will come in March and April.

**Price for the current market, not the spring peak you hope for.** Sellers who listed in October 2026 expecting spring 2022 pricing are still on the market. The revised outlook is more optimistic than Q3 2026 data alone would suggest, but sellers who overprice in January will be correcting in March.

**Prepare the home for maximum internet appeal.** Professional photography, light staging, and a pre-listing inspection allow you to get ahead of the buyer's inspection contingency and present a move-in-ready product to buyers who want to close quickly.

**Understand your portability.** If you plan to sell a Valrico home and buy another Florida home, file your portability application (Form DR-501T) with the Hillsborough County Property Appraiser at the same time you file your new homestead exemption. The three-year window is generous but the March 1 annual deadline is firm.

## What Buyers Should Do Before the Spring 2027 Market

The revised 2027 outlook suggests the spring market in Valrico, particularly in 33596, will be more competitive than 2026's summer and fall were. Buyers who start in November or December 2026 have advantages over buyers who start in March 2027.

**Close before December 31 if at all possible.** The Amendment 3 savings start January 1, 2027. A December close captures the first year of savings.

**Get pre-approved at current rates and re-lock when rates move lower.** Many lenders allow you to float your rate and lock at a lower point before closing. If you are pre-approved now at 6.5 percent and rates drop to 6.0 percent in January 2027, you can re-lock and benefit.

**Target 33596 Newsome zone homes in the $430,000 to $480,000 range.** This is the highest-demand segment in 2027's forecast. Waiting for a price correction in this zone is not supported by the data. Competing listings in spring 2027 will give you an opportunity cost problem if you wait.

**Use seller concessions now, while they are still available.** Sellers accepting closing cost contributions and rate buydowns are commonplace in November 2026. That flexibility narrows as competition increases in spring 2027.

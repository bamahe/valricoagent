## Amendment 3 Takes Effect January 1, 2027: What It Means in Dollars

Florida voters approved Amendment 3 on November 3, 2026 with 62 percent of the vote. The amendment expands the non-school homestead property tax exemption from $25,000 to $150,000 effective January 1, 2027. In 2028, it expands again to $250,000 with inflation adjustments from 2029 onward.

For Valrico homeowners, the annual savings are substantial:

**33594 (Bloomingdale, Twin Lakes, Copper Ridge)**
- Median assessed value: approximately $375,000 to $382,000
- Current non-school taxable value with existing exemptions: approximately $325,000
- 2027 taxable value after Amendment 3: approximately $225,000
- Estimated annual tax savings: approximately $950 to $1,050 per year (using Hillsborough County non-school millage rates)

**33596 (Buckhorn, River Hills, Diamond Hill, Newsome Zone)**
- Median assessed value: approximately $462,000 to $474,000
- Current non-school taxable value with existing exemptions: approximately $412,000
- 2027 taxable value after Amendment 3: approximately $312,000
- Estimated annual tax savings: approximately $1,050 to $1,150 per year

For buyers closing after December 31, 2026, they can still receive the Amendment 3 benefit for the 2027 tax year -- but only if they establish homestead exemption by March 1, 2027. Missing the March 1 deadline means waiting until 2028 to receive the benefit, costing a first-year loss of $950 to $1,150 depending on assessed value.

For perspective on the December 31 urgency: a buyer who closes January 15, 2027 instead of December 29, 2026 is not necessarily worse off financially -- they can still file for homestead exemption by March 1 and receive full 2027 savings. The critical factor is not December 31; it is the March 1, 2027 filing deadline at the Hillsborough County Property Appraiser's office.

The practical closing timeline: purchases typically take 25 to 35 days from accepted offer to close. Offers accepted in late November and December 2026 can still close in time to file homestead by March 1, 2027. Buyers purchasing in January should close as early as possible in the month to allow time for the homestead application.

## January 2027 Market Conditions: What to Expect in Valrico

January is historically the slowest month in Valrico's real estate market. Here is what the data tells us about typical January conditions and how 2027 is likely to look.

### Buyer Competition

January buyer activity in Hillsborough County runs 30 to 40 percent below peak spring levels. Families with school-age children typically wait until late February or March before entering the market in force. That lower competition translates to real leverage for buyers willing to act in January:

- Sellers who listed in fall 2026 and have not sold are acutely motivated by January
- Year-end carryover listings have often been on the market 90-plus days with multiple price reductions
- January 2027 specifically benefits from the Amendment 3 urgency among buyers who want homestead by March 1

Expect to find motivated sellers, meaningful concession opportunities, and significantly less competition from other buyers compared to April or May 2027.

### Inventory Levels

Active listings in Valrico reached approximately 240 to 260 homes in late October and November 2026 -- the highest inventory levels since 2018 to 2019. That inventory typically carries into January. Some homes will sell in December to year-end motivated buyers. New listings slow to a trickle in December but pick up again after January 10.

The net effect: January 2027 buyers will likely face 200 to 230 active listings in Valrico, with a meaningful portion having 60-plus days on market and sellers who are increasingly willing to negotiate.

### Pricing in January

33594 (Bloomingdale corridor and surrounding areas) has shown flat to down 1 to 2 percent year-over-year pricing in Q4 2026. That trend is expected to hold through January, with the spring market bringing stabilization or modest appreciation beginning in March to April 2027.

33596 (Buckhorn, River Hills, Newsome zone) has outperformed 33594 throughout 2026, running flat to up 1 percent year-over-year. The Newsome High School attendance zone creates persistent demand that buffers price softening. That dynamic continues into January 2027.

Buyers should use current market data, not 2024 or 2025 comps, when making offers in January 2027. Sold prices from 6 to 12 months ago are no longer accurate benchmarks in a market that has normalized from the 2021 to 2024 surge.

## Mortgage Rate Outlook for Q1 2027

Mortgage rates running 6.80 to 7.40 percent in November 2026 are the primary constraint on buyer purchasing power. Here is what buyers and sellers need to understand about the rate environment entering January 2027.

### The Fed's December 2026 Meeting

The Federal Reserve's Federal Open Market Committee is scheduled to meet in mid-December 2026. The Fed funds rate directly influences the short end of the yield curve, while mortgage rates are more closely tied to the 10-year Treasury yield and bond market expectations. That said, Fed policy signals matter for buyer confidence and lender rate setting.

Going into the December 2026 meeting, consensus among economists tracked by the Chicago Mercantile Exchange FedWatch tool generally expects the Fed to remain in a "hold" or modest easing posture, with any rate cut of 25 basis points or less having a limited direct effect on 30-year mortgage rates. Bond markets, which ultimately price mortgage rates, are watching inflation data, employment, and Fed guidance simultaneously.

**Scenario 1 -- Fed holds and signals no near-term cuts:** Mortgage rates remain in the 6.80 to 7.00 percent range through Q1 2027. Buyer pool stays constrained to those qualifying at current rates. Market continues favoring buyers.

**Scenario 2 -- Fed cuts 25 basis points and signals further easing:** Mortgage rates could drift toward 6.50 to 6.75 percent by late Q1 2027. A 25-basis-point cut on a $400,000 loan reduces monthly principal and interest by approximately $65 to $75. Not transformative, but it widens the qualifying buyer pool modestly.

**Scenario 3 -- Inflation data surprises and rates rise:** Mortgage rates could move toward 7.25 to 7.50 percent. Buyer pool contracts further. This scenario increases seller concession pressure.

### What Buyers Should Do About Rates

Rate-lock strategy for January 2027 purchasers:

1. **Get pre-approved at current rates, not anticipated future rates.** Underwrite your purchase based on what rates are today, not what you hope they will be.
2. **Ask about float-down options.** Some lenders offer float-down provisions that allow you to lock at current rates but capture a lower rate if rates fall by a minimum amount before closing. These typically cost 0.25 to 0.5 percent upfront.
3. **Consider seller buydowns.** In a buyer's market, requesting a 1-0 or 2-1 buydown paid by the seller reduces your effective rate in the first year or two. On a $400,000 loan, a 1-0 buydown reduces your year-one rate by 1 percent, saving approximately $265 per month in year one.
4. **Don't wait for 6 percent rates.** Waiting for a specific rate level to buy in Valrico has historically cost buyers more in home price appreciation than they saved in rate savings. The best rate environment to buy in is the one where you can afford the payment and find a home that meets your needs.

## Seller Strategy for January 2027

January sellers in Valrico face a different tactical environment than December.

**The holiday slowdown is real but brief.** Between Thanksgiving and New Year's Day, buyer activity drops significantly. The first two weeks of January are still slower than typical. Active buyer traffic picks up meaningfully by January 15 and accelerates toward peak in March to May.

**Sellers who waited out December have a window.** Buyers who could not close by December 31 are still motivated by the March 1 homestead deadline. That urgency means January 2027 is not a dead month -- it is an opportunity for sellers who price correctly.

**New listings surge in late January.** The spring listing inventory influx begins in January, with many sellers timing their list date for late January or early February. For sellers who list in the first two weeks of January, they have a brief window of reduced competition before the spring inventory wave hits.

**Pricing in January.** The November 2026 Valrico median is approximately $413,000 blended across both ZIPs. Sellers should not expect January to bring higher prices than November. Buyers entering in January have access to full November comps and will negotiate against them. Price at or just below comparables from October and November 2026 to generate interest quickly. Overpricing in January, then chasing the market down, is the fastest way to sell at a lower final price.

## The Homestead Exemption Filing Checklist

Buyers closing on a Valrico home in December 2026 or January 2027 must file for homestead exemption with the Hillsborough County Property Appraiser to receive the 2027 benefit -- including the Amendment 3 expanded exemption. The deadline is March 1, 2027.

**What you need to file:**
- Florida driver's license or state ID with your new Valrico address
- Florida vehicle registration with your new address (or equivalent documentation)
- Social Security number for all owners
- Proof of ownership (warranty deed or closing disclosure)

**How to file:**
- In person: Hillsborough County Property Appraiser's office, 601 E Kennedy Blvd, Tampa, FL 33602
- Online: hcpafl.org offers online filing with documentation upload
- By mail: Form DR-501 to the Property Appraiser's office

**Save Our Homes portability:** If you are a Valrico seller moving to a new Florida home, you may be eligible to transfer your accumulated Save Our Homes tax benefit to the new property. The portability application (Form DR-501T) must also be filed by March 1, 2027. Under existing law, the portable amount is capped at $500,000 of accumulated Save Our Homes differential. For more detail, see our [Florida Save Our Homes portability guide for Valrico sellers](https://valricoagent.com/blog/florida-save-our-homes-portability-valrico-sellers-guide/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When does Florida Amendment 3 take effect for property taxes?**
Amendment 3 takes effect January 1, 2027 for the 2027 tax year. Homeowners will see the expanded $150,000 non-school homestead exemption reflected in their 2027 TRIM notice, typically mailed in August 2027. The savings apply automatically to any home with an active homestead exemption on January 1, 2027. Homeowners who establish homestead by filing with the Hillsborough County Property Appraiser before March 1, 2027 will receive the full 2027 benefit.

**What is the homestead exemption deadline in Hillsborough County for 2027?**
The homestead exemption application deadline is March 1, 2027. Buyers who close on a Valrico home in December 2026 or January 2027 must file Form DR-501 with the Hillsborough County Property Appraiser at hcpafl.org by March 1 to receive the 2027 expanded exemption. Missing this deadline means waiting until the 2028 tax year for the Amendment 3 savings.

**Is January 2027 a good time to buy a home in Valrico FL?**
January 2027 offers meaningful advantages for buyers: less competition than spring, motivated sellers who have been on market since fall 2026, inventory of 200-plus homes, and significant negotiating leverage. The March 1 homestead deadline creates a window of buyer activity that is more active than a typical January. The tradeoff is that mortgage rates remain in the 6.80 to 7.40 percent range, constraining monthly payment affordability. Buyers who plan carefully, underwrite conservatively, and ask for seller concessions can find strong value in January 2027.

**Will Valrico home prices drop in January 2027?**
Significant price drops in January 2027 are not anticipated. The market is in normalization mode, not distress. 33594 median prices are flat to down 1 to 2 percent year-over-year, while 33596 is flat to up 1 percent. January historically does not bring significant price movement in either direction. The bigger factors for price direction in 2027 are mortgage rate changes and whether the spring buyer market materializes. Buyers should not wait for a price drop that is unlikely to exceed the 1 to 2 percent range, since any rate decrease would push prices back up by a comparable amount.

**How much can a Valrico buyer save with Amendment 3 in 2027?**
Most Valrico homeowners and buyers will see approximately $950 to $1,150 in annual property tax savings in 2027 under Amendment 3. For 33594 homes assessed at $375,000 to $382,000, the annual savings run approximately $950 to $1,050. For 33596 Newsome zone homes assessed at $462,000 to $474,000, the savings run approximately $1,050 to $1,150. These translate to roughly $79 to $96 per month in reduced carrying cost. In 2028, when the exemption expands to $250,000, the annual savings increase to approximately $2,000 to $2,500 for most Valrico homeowners.

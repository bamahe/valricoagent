Spring Hill, Florida sits in Hernando County roughly 40 to 45 miles north of downtown Tampa, at the point where Hillsborough County's suburban density gives way to a more rural, lower-density landscape. For buyers comparing options across the greater Tampa Bay region, Spring Hill represents one of the most affordable large-scale suburban markets within commuting distance of Tampa. This guide breaks down the 2026 Spring Hill real estate market by ZIP code, covers what you actually pay to own here, and gives you an honest comparison with Valrico so you can decide which market fits your priorities.

## Spring Hill Market Overview: 2026 By the Numbers

Spring Hill is not a single market. Its six ZIP codes serve different buyer profiles, and the price gap between the most affordable and most expensive ZIP can be $125,000 or more. Here is what the data shows as of Q4 2026.

**34606 (South Spring Hill, Weeki Wachee border)**

The southernmost Spring Hill ZIP has the lowest median in the city. List prices are clustering around $270,000 to $285,000, with 12-month sold data showing a median near $300,000. Days on market runs approximately 33 to 45 days for move-in-ready homes at or below the median. This ZIP includes older 1980s and 1990s subdivisions on the Weeki Wachee side and a mix of canal and non-canal lots near the river system.

**34607 (Spring Hill coastal fringe, Pine Island)**

This ZIP includes some Gulf-adjacent and canal-access properties near Pine Island and the Spring Hill coast. Prices vary more here because of waterfront premium properties. The Gulf Coast lifestyle access from this ZIP is distinct from purely inland Spring Hill and carries insurance costs to match. Buyers considering coastal properties in 34607 should budget for flood zone verification before making any offer.

**34608 (Central Spring Hill, Timber Pines)**

The largest Spring Hill ZIP by transaction volume. Median sale price is running approximately $299,900 to $315,000 in 2026, down roughly 1 to 2% year over year according to Momentum Realty data. Days on market averages 32 to 45 days. This ZIP includes Timber Pines, a popular 55-plus golf community, and a wide range of standard subdivisions from 1980s construction through 2000s builds.

Price per square foot in 34608 is running approximately $165 to $195 for single-family homes, reflecting the older housing stock and larger lot sizes relative to newer Tampa suburbs.

**34609 (North Spring Hill, newer development corridor)**

The 34609 ZIP is Spring Hill's growth corridor. Its median list price is tracking around $359,900 with 12-month sold data near $374,900. Days on market is approximately 55 to 65 days, longer than the southern ZIPs because this tier attracts more comparison shoppers cross-shopping with Wesley Chapel, New Port Richey, and other Pasco County alternatives.

Newer subdivisions in 34609 include options built in the 2010s and 2020s with better energy efficiency, updated floor plans, and current building codes. This ZIP has the most first-time builder construction of the Spring Hill ZIPs and attracts buyers who want the price point of Hernando County without buying a 1985 home.

**34610 and 34611 (Eastern Spring Hill, Ridge Road corridor)**

34610 is the easternmost ZIP and carries a slightly higher median near $399,000, reflecting newer construction along the Ridge Road corridor and proximity to Wesley Chapel via SR-54. This ZIP straddles the Pasco-Hernando border area in terms of buyer psychology and comparables.

**Spring Hill citywide 2026:**
- Overall median sale price: approximately $300,000 to $345,000 depending on source and trailing period
- Active listings: approximately 370 to 403 homes for sale as of October 2026
- Months of supply: approximately 3.5 to 4.5 months, balanced to slightly buyer-favorable
- Year-over-year price change: down 1 to 6% depending on ZIP and metric used, with Redfin's city-level data showing approximately negative 5.8% over the three-month trailing period ending September 2026
- Median days on market: approximately 45 to 87 days across sources (the range reflects different measurement methods; list-to-contract is closer to 40 to 55 days for well-priced homes)

## Spring Hill Neighborhoods: What Buyers Need to Know

### Timber Pines (34608): The 55-Plus Golf Community

Timber Pines is one of Spring Hill's most established communities and consistently among its most active by transaction volume. This 55-plus development offers multiple golf courses, a performing arts theater, fitness facilities, tennis courts, and a strong roster of planned activities that rivals larger retirement communities in Sarasota and Pasco County.

Homes in Timber Pines range from smaller villas around $185,000 to $225,000 up to larger single-family homes at $310,000 to $380,000 depending on lot position, age, and updates. The HOA fee in Timber Pines covers common amenities and runs approximately $155 to $185 per month. This is one of the more affordable active-adult communities in the broader Tampa Bay area for the amenity level it delivers.

For buyers considering a downsize from Valrico or east Hillsborough, Timber Pines is worth a direct comparison with Valrico's 55-plus options. The Spring Hill price point is roughly $100,000 to $150,000 lower on comparable square footage, but the commute to Tampa lengthens substantially and school zone considerations disappear.

### Silverthorn (34609): Private Golf in North Spring Hill

Silverthorn is a gated golf community in the 34609 ZIP with a Harborside Golf Course as its centerpiece. Home prices in Silverthorn run $295,000 to $495,000 depending on size, view, and updates. The gated entry and golf community amenities attract buyers who want a lifestyle community without the Pasco County price premium of comparable communities like Heritage Pines in Trinity or Esplanade at Wesley Chapel.

HOA fees in Silverthorn are approximately $125 to $200 per month depending on the specific section. No CDD. This is a meaningful differentiator against the Wesley Chapel market, where CDD assessments of $1,500 to $4,000 per year on top of HOA fees add $125 to $330 per month in non-optional carrying costs.

### Newer Subdivisions Along Ridge Road and Spring Hill Drive (34609)

The north corridor of Spring Hill has seen the most recent residential development activity. Builders including D.R. Horton, Maronda Homes, and LGI Homes have delivered subdivisions in this corridor in the 2015 to 2024 window. These homes offer more contemporary floor plans, energy-efficient construction, and move-in-ready condition that appeals to buyers who want newer inventory without paying Pasco County or Hillsborough County prices.

Homes in this corridor start around $299,900 and run to $425,000 for larger 4 and 5-bedroom plans. The trade-off versus older Spring Hill subdivisions is that these neighborhoods have less tree canopy, smaller lots, and HOA fees where applicable.

## Property Taxes in Hernando County vs Hillsborough County

One of the most significant carrying cost differences between Spring Hill and Valrico is property taxes. Hernando County's millage rate is meaningfully lower than Hillsborough County's.

**Hernando County (Spring Hill):**
- Effective tax rate on homesteaded properties: approximately 0.85% to 1.05% of assessed value
- On a $300,000 assessed value with homestead exemption: approximately $2,100 to $2,550 per year
- Hernando County has no separate school board CDD or non-ad valorem assessments in most of Spring Hill

**Hillsborough County (Valrico):**
- Effective tax rate on homesteaded properties: approximately 1.35% to 1.55% of assessed value
- On a $413,000 assessed value with homestead exemption: approximately $4,500 to $5,500 per year

The tax differential is real and meaningful. A Spring Hill homeowner at $300,000 assessed pays roughly $2,500 less per year in property taxes than a Valrico homeowner at $413,000 assessed. Even adjusting for the lower purchase price in Spring Hill, the tax rate difference adds up over a 10-year holding period. For buyers factoring total cost of ownership, not just mortgage payment, this gap matters.

**Homestead exemption in Florida:** Applies in both counties with the same mechanics. File by March 1 after your closing year to lock in your exemption for that tax year. The $50,000 homestead exemption and the 3% Save Our Homes cap on annual assessment increases apply identically in Hernando County as in Hillsborough County.

## Homeowners Insurance in Spring Hill: The Full Picture

Insurance costs in Spring Hill vary by ZIP code and specific flood zone, but the general picture compares favorably to coastal Pinellas and Hillsborough markets.

**Inland Spring Hill (34608, 34609):**
- HO-3 policy for a standard 1,800 to 2,200 square foot home: approximately $2,200 to $3,500 per year
- Most of inland Spring Hill is in FEMA Flood Zone X, with minimal flood risk and no mandatory flood insurance requirement
- Wind is the primary driver of premium, not flood, for most Spring Hill properties

**Coastal and canal Spring Hill (34607, portions of 34606):**
- HO-3 policy: approximately $3,500 to $5,500 per year
- Flood insurance required for Zone AE properties near the Gulf: $1,500 to $4,500 per year under NFIP Risk Rating 2.0
- Canal-front and Gulf-adjacent properties require detailed flood zone verification before making any offer

**Comparison to Valrico:** Inland Valrico runs approximately $2,800 to $4,200 per year for a standard HO-3 policy. Spring Hill inland insurance tends to be modestly lower, partly because of lower home values and partly because Hernando County sits slightly further north of the main Tampa Bay hurricane corridor.

The practical note for buyers: always get a current insurance quote from a licensed Florida agent before closing. The market for Florida homeowners insurance has shifted materially in 2024 through 2026, and national carrier withdrawal has pushed many Florida homeowners to Citizens Insurance or smaller admitted carriers. This is true in both Valrico and Spring Hill.

## Schools: Hernando County School District vs Hillsborough County

School zone quality is one of the most significant differences between Spring Hill and Valrico, and it is honest to address it directly.

**Hernando County School District:**
The Hernando County School District serves Spring Hill. High schools include Springstead High School and Central High School for most of the city. The district's academic performance is generally rated lower than Hillsborough County's top zones and significantly lower than Newsome High School in Valrico's 33596 ZIP.

For families who do not have school-age children or whose children are already in high school, this difference is largely academic. For families with children entering middle or high school, the school zone gap between Spring Hill and Newsome-zone Valrico is a meaningful quality-of-life consideration.

**Hillsborough County (Valrico):**
Valrico's 33596 ZIP feeds into Newsome High School, consistently rated among Hillsborough County's top high schools for academic performance, AP course offerings, and college placement. The 33594 ZIP feeds primarily into Bloomingdale High School, which is also rated solid if not at Newsome's level. For many Valrico buyers, the school zone premium is the single largest driver of their decision to pay Hillsborough County prices.

## Commute Times: Spring Hill to Tampa

This is the biggest practical trade-off for buyers comparing Spring Hill to closer-in suburbs.

**Spring Hill to downtown Tampa:** Approximately 40 to 60 miles depending on the specific starting point in Spring Hill. Drive time ranges from 55 to 85 minutes under normal conditions via US-19 or the Suncoast Parkway (toll). During peak rush hour, the Suncoast adds toll costs of approximately $5 to $10 per day if used in both directions, or approximately $2,600 per year for a daily commuter.

**Spring Hill to Wesley Chapel / Wiregrass area:** Approximately 25 to 35 miles, 35 to 50 minutes via SR-54 or County Line Road. This makes Spring Hill more practical for workers in the Pasco County employment corridor than for Tampa-based commuters.

**Spring Hill to Brandon / east Hillsborough:** Not a practical daily commute. The drive from central Spring Hill to Brandon runs 50 to 70 miles and 70 to 100 minutes under most conditions.

**Valrico to downtown Tampa for comparison:** Approximately 18 to 25 miles, 30 to 50 minutes via I-4 or SR-60. Valrico's commute advantage over Spring Hill is meaningful for Tampa-based workers and roughly equal for Pasco County-based employment.

The remote work factor: approximately 30 to 40% of Spring Hill's residential buyer inquiries involve some combination of remote or hybrid work. For buyers who work remotely two to three days per week and commute two to three days, Spring Hill's commute is more manageable. Full-time five-day Tampa commuters will feel the distance.

## What Does Your Budget Buy in Spring Hill vs Valrico?

Here is a concrete side-by-side at four common price points.

**At $300,000:**
- Spring Hill: A 3-bedroom, 2-bath home in the 1,400 to 1,800 square foot range, likely built between 1985 and 2005, in an established subdivision. Move-in ready at this price point is achievable. Many options in 34608 and the south 34609 corridor.
- Valrico: At $300,000, you are in distressed, heavily dated, or unusual circumstances. The Valrico median is $413,000 combined, and $300,000 homes in Valrico require significant work or are very small.

**At $380,000:**
- Spring Hill: A newer 4-bedroom, 2-bath home in the 1,900 to 2,300 square foot range, likely 2015 construction or newer, in a well-maintained subdivision with some amenities. The 34609 corridor has options at this price with good condition.
- Valrico: A 3-bedroom, 2-bath home in Bloomingdale or Twin Lakes, 1,800 to 2,200 square feet, older 1990s-2000s construction. Move-in ready is possible but competitive. You are entering the most contested segment of the Valrico market.

**At $450,000:**
- Spring Hill: A larger custom-style or upscale production home, 2,400 to 3,000 square feet, potentially with golf course views in Silverthorn or comparable. At this price in Spring Hill, buyers are getting significantly more home per dollar than any comparable Tampa suburb.
- Valrico: A 4-bedroom home in the 2,000 to 2,600 square foot range in 33594, or entry to the 33596 ZIP for smaller or older Buckhorn homes. You are competitive in Bloomingdale Estates territory. Newsome zone starts becoming accessible at this price point.

## Investment Perspective: Spring Hill vs Valrico

For investors, Spring Hill and Valrico serve different strategies.

**Gross rental yield comparison:**
- Spring Hill (34608, median $300K, estimated monthly rent $1,600 to $1,850 for a 3-bedroom): Gross yield approximately 6.4% to 7.4%
- Valrico 33594 (median $378K, estimated monthly rent $2,200 to $2,450): Gross yield approximately 6.9% to 7.8%

On pure yield, the numbers are similar. The Valrico advantage is lower vacancy risk driven by school zone demand and proximity to Tampa employment. The Spring Hill advantage is lower purchase price and lower property taxes, which improves net cash flow for buy-and-hold investors.

The risk factor to watch in Spring Hill: insurance and property tax increases are less predictable in Hernando County than in Hillsborough, and the market's smaller employment base creates more tenant sensitivity to regional economic shifts. Long-term appreciation in Valrico has historically outpaced Spring Hill, reflecting the school zone premium and Hillsborough County's stronger employment base.

Spring Hill: $165 to $195 per square foot for resale in 34608 and 34609
Valrico: $185 to $232 per square foot depending on ZIP and condition

**3. Commute to Tampa**
Spring Hill: 55 to 85 minutes, 40 to 60 miles
Valrico: 30 to 50 minutes, 18 to 25 miles

**4. Property taxes**

Neither is better in absolute terms. This trade-off is personal. Buyers who want space, quiet, and a lower cost of ownership will often prefer Spring Hill. Buyers who want school zone access, Tampa proximity, and established neighborhood infrastructure tend to prefer Valrico.

## Q4 2026 Buyer Strategy for Spring Hill

If you are seriously considering Spring Hill in the current market, here is a practical framework.

**Target the 34609 corridor for newer construction value.** The north Spring Hill ZIP has the best inventory of post-2010 homes in move-in condition, with prices still below comparable-vintage homes in Hillsborough and Pasco County.

**Separate Timber Pines from the general Spring Hill market.** If you are 55 or older, Timber Pines deserves its own separate evaluation as an active adult community. Compare it directly against Sun City Center and Heritage Pines in Trinity for amenity value and HOA cost structure.

**Run flood zone and insurance quotes on every Hernando coastal property before making an offer.** The 34607 ZIP and portions of 34606 near the Gulf of Mexico have flood zone exposure that can make carrying costs unworkable. Zone X properties in central Spring Hill do not have this issue.

**Use the current buyer's market conditions.** With 3.5 to 4.5 months of supply and days on market averaging 45 to 65 days in most ZIPs, buyers have real negotiating room. Closing cost contributions of $5,000 to $8,000 are achievable on mid-range homes, particularly on listings that have been active 45 days or longer.

The question comes up more often now: Valrico or Wimauma? Both are in Hillsborough County, both have active real estate markets heading into 2027, and both offer suburban living with access to Tampa. But they are genuinely different places with different price points, different amenity profiles, different school zones, and different community characters. Here is the honest comparison buyers need.

## Price: Wimauma Is Cheaper, But the Gap Narrows with Fees

The most obvious difference is price. As of late 2026, the median closed sale price in Wimauma 33598 is approximately $350,000, based on recent transaction data. Valrico's blended median across 33594 and 33596 sits at approximately $413,000. That is roughly a $60,000 to $65,000 gap in median price.

However, the gap closes significantly once you factor in Community Development District fees and HOA assessments, which are prevalent in Wimauma's newer master-planned communities.

**Wimauma true cost example:**
- Home price: $355,000
- CDD fee: approximately $2,000 to $3,000 per year (billed through property tax)
- HOA fee: approximately $150 to $200 per month in communities like SouthShore Bay or Sereno

Adding $2,500 per year in CDD fees and $175 per month in HOA to the carrying cost adds approximately $450 per month to the effective monthly payment on a Wimauma home compared to what the base mortgage suggests.

**Valrico true cost example:**
- Home price: $395,000 (comparable-sized 3-4 bedroom in 33594)
- CDD fee: none in most established Bloomingdale, Buckhorn, and Twin Lakes homes
- HOA fee: $0 to $75 per month in most Valrico subdivisions

The adjusted monthly cost difference between a comparable home in Valrico and a new-construction home in Wimauma's master-planned communities is often $200 to $350 per month, not $500 or more as the raw list prices suggest.

## School Zones: Valrico Holds the Advantage in Secondary Education

For families with children, school zone is frequently the deciding factor between Valrico and Wimauma.

**Valrico school zones:**
- 33596 (River Hills, Buckhorn, Diamond Hill): Zoned for Newsome High School, ranked among the top public high schools in Hillsborough County with an A rating and consistent recognition for academic performance.
- 33594 (Bloomingdale, Copper Ridge, Twin Lakes): Primarily zoned for Bloomingdale Senior High, a solid school that does not carry the same premium as Newsome.
- Middle school: Mulrennan Middle serves 33596; McLane Middle serves much of 33594. Both are rated above average.

**Wimauma school zones:**
- Sumner High School in Riverview serves a portion of Wimauma 33598. Sumner is a newer school that has not yet built the academic reputation track record that Newsome has accumulated. It is too early to draw firm comparisons.
- Some Wimauma properties feed to other Hillsborough County high schools depending on exact address.
- Elementary: Wimauma Community School serves parts of 33598. The rating is lower than what Valrico families typically see at Corr, Cimino, or Lithia Springs elementary.

Always verify your specific address on the [Hillsborough County Schools boundary locator](https://www.sdhc.k12.fl.us/). School zones in Hillsborough County boundaries can change, and addresses near zone boundaries may have different assignments.

**The practical impact:** Valrico 33596 carries a school zone premium that is reflected in home prices. The Newsome HS zone pushes 33596 medians to approximately $441,000 to $452,000, versus Valrico 33594 at approximately $367,000 to $382,000. Families who specifically need Newsome zone access should focus their Valrico search on 33596 and understand the premium is real.

## Amenities: Wimauma Has the Crystal Lagoon, Valrico Has Established Community Character

This is where the communities diverge most visibly. Wimauma's growth story centers on resort-style amenities that do not exist in Valrico.

**SouthShore Bay** features a 5.5-acre Crystal Lagoon, a privately operated man-made water amenity with swim areas, paddleboards, and waterfront event spaces. This is a genuine differentiator. If you want a lagoon-style amenity at your home community, it exists in Wimauma and not in Valrico.

**Sereno** and **Berry Bay** offer community pools and amenity centers consistent with newer master-planned community standards.

**Valencia Lakes** is a 55-plus community in Wimauma with an established social infrastructure: tennis, pickleball, fitness center, pools, and extensive programming for active adults.

Valrico does not offer comparable resort-style amenities in a single master-planned package. What it offers instead:

- **River Hills Country Club:** A private golf club community with a golf course, tennis courts, and pool. Membership is separate from home purchase. River Hills is an established community (most homes built 1990s to 2000s) with mature trees and a neighborhood character that takes decades to develop.
- **Bloomingdale Golf Course:** Public access golf within the community.
- **Proximity to existing Hillsborough County parks** including Alafia River State Park, Lithia Springs, and Alderman's Ford Regional Park for outdoor recreation.
- **Established retail and dining** at Bloomingdale Avenue, US-301, and SR-60 corridors with no need to drive to major road infrastructure that is still being built.

The honest trade-off: Wimauma buyers get newer homes, resort-style amenities, and lower base prices. Valrico buyers get established community character, mature tree canopy, existing retail and infrastructure, and in 33596, a proven school zone track record.

## New Construction Availability: Both Markets Have It, Wimauma Has More

Valrico's new construction market in late 2026 includes:
- **Pulte Homes at Valri Forest** (33594): Starting from approximately $416,990 for 3-4 bedroom floor plans.
- **Homes by WestBay at Northwood Estates** (33596): Starting from approximately $531,990, targeting buyers who want a larger home in the Newsome school zone.
- Limited in-fill lots and builder spec homes in various subdivisions.

Wimauma's new construction pipeline is significantly larger. Multiple national builders including D.R. Horton at Berry Bay and Sereno, Lennar at Creek Preserve, and several others are actively building in 33598. The variety of price points and product types in Wimauma new construction is broader than what is available in Valrico, particularly in the $300,000 to $400,000 range where Valrico has limited new construction.

Buyers who specifically want new construction and want the most options at the lowest entry point should consider Wimauma seriously. ## Commute to Tampa: Valrico Is Closer

This is a meaningful practical difference for buyers who commute to Tampa.

**Valrico to downtown Tampa:** Approximately 25 to 35 minutes via SR-60 to I-75 or I-4, or via the Selmon Expressway from Brandon. During peak commute hours, 35 to 50 minutes is more realistic.

**Wimauma to downtown Tampa:** Approximately 40 to 55 minutes via US-301 to I-75, or the same via I-75 south-to-north routing. During peak hours, 50 to 70 minutes is realistic.

The difference is not trivial for daily commuters. An extra 15 to 20 minutes each way is 2.5 to 3+ hours per week. Over a year that adds up. Buyers who place high weight on commute time and are considering both areas should factor this in honestly.

**Tampa-adjacent employment corridors served better by Valrico:** Brandon/Westfield, MacDill AFB via Gandy Bridge, East Tampa/Seminole Heights, Ybor City and Channel District.

**Employment corridors where Wimauma is competitive:** Brandon/Riverview (via US-301), Ruskin/Sun City Center (immediate), and buyers whose employers are on the south-side of Tampa Bay or in Sarasota County.

For MacDill AFB buyers specifically, Valrico is a popular choice with established communities and reasonable commute time. See the [VA Loan and MacDill AFB Buyer Guide](/blog/va-loan-home-buying-valrico-fl-macdill-afb-guide/) for how that commute and benefit structure plays out.

## Flood Risk and Insurance

Both Valrico and Wimauma are predominantly in FEMA Flood Zone X (minimal flood hazard), which means flood insurance is not required by lenders in most areas. Neither community is in a coastal flood risk zone.

Wimauma has some areas near the Little Manatee River with higher flood zone designations. Buyers in Wimauma should check the specific parcel address on the [FEMA Flood Map Service Center](https://msc.fema.gov) before purchasing.

Homeowners insurance rates in both areas have increased significantly in Florida since 2022. Expect annual premiums in the $3,000 to $5,500 range for a mid-size single-family home in either community in 2026-2027. The difference between Valrico and Wimauma on insurance cost is not a meaningful factor in the comparison. Location within Florida matters more than the choice between these two specific communities.

## Investor and Rental Market

Both Valrico and Wimauma have active rental markets, but the dynamics differ.

**Valrico rental market:**
- Rents for a 3-4 bedroom single-family home range approximately $2,100 to $2,600 per month.
- Strong demand from families specifically seeking Newsome HS zone access who are not ready to buy.
- Lower vacancy rates in 33596 versus 33594.
- Cap rates: approximately 4.5 to 5.5 percent net for well-priced acquisitions.

**Wimauma rental market:**
- Lower acquisition cost allows for comparable or slightly higher gross yields.
- SouthShore Bay and other master-planned communities have CDD and HOA fees that reduce net returns.
- New construction rental demand is strong from relocating families.
- Some landlords in Wimauma deal with longer vacancy periods when competing against new construction rentals in the same community.

For investors with a strict return-first mandate, Wimauma's lower acquisition cost is attractive. For investors who prioritize stable long-term demand, tenant quality, and low vacancy, Valrico's school zone premium tends to support both.

## Who Should Buy in Valrico vs Wimauma

**Buy in Valrico if:**
- School zone access is a priority, especially Newsome HS in 33596.
- You prefer established neighborhoods with mature tree canopy and a quieter suburban character.
- Your commute destination is Tampa-adjacent or MacDill AFB.
- You want to avoid CDD fees and high HOA costs.
- You are buying resale and want a broad comp market with proven price history.

**Buy in Wimauma if:**
- Resort-style amenities, especially the SouthShore Bay Crystal Lagoon, matter to you.
- New construction is a priority and you want more product variety at a lower entry price.
- You are 55-plus and interested in Valencia Lakes or the Medley section of SouthShore Bay.
- Your employer is in Ruskin, Sun City Center, or south Hillsborough County.
- You can absorb the CDD and HOA fees and still hit your monthly budget.

## The 2027 Outlook for Both Markets

Valrico is expected to see modest appreciation of 2 to 3 percent in 2027, underpinned by Amendment 3 property tax savings that took effect in 2027, persistent Newsome HS zone demand, and limited new supply in established neighborhoods.

Wimauma's new construction pipeline will continue to supply the market through 2027, which keeps appreciation moderate but supports transaction volume. Buyers in Wimauma in 2027 should expect continued builder incentives as new phases release.

Both markets are healthy. The choice comes down to your priorities, not which market is objectively better.

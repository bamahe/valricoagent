## North River Ranch: The Engine of Parrish Growth

No conversation about Parrish real estate in 2026 is complete without North River Ranch. This 2,440-acre master-planned community along Fort Hamer Road has fundamentally reshaped the 34219 market, bringing thousands of new homes and national builder inventory that did not exist five years ago.

**Homes.com data for North River Ranch:** Average home value of $522,326, with an average price per square foot of $226. With 86 homes currently listed, it is the most active new construction community in the Parrish market.

**Active builders in North River Ranch include:**
- **David Weekley Homes:** Garden series and townhomes, priced from approximately $450,000 to $580,000. Known for well-designed floor plans and solid construction quality.
- **Lennar:** Multiple series ranging from entry-level to executive homes, $420,000 to $600,000+.
- **D.R. Horton:** Value-focused production builder with homes starting in the $380,000 to $440,000 range. D.R. Horton's pricing creates the entry point into the community.
- **Taylor Morrison and Neal Communities:** Mid-to-upper price range homes with more architectural variety and larger lot options, $500,000 to $700,000+.

**Community amenities inside North River Ranch:** The community is designed to function as a self-contained neighborhood with parks, playgrounds, trails, resort-style amenity centers, and pool facilities. The scale of the community creates a walkable feel that is unusual for this part of Manatee County.

**Schools within the community:** North River Ranch has its own school infrastructure. Barbara A. Harvey Elementary opened within the community. A new middle school, Harvey North River Ranch Middle School, was under construction for the 2025 to 2026 school year. North River Charter Academy is opening a new campus in September 2026 at 13020 Rutland Road, starting with kindergarten through sixth grade and expanding to seventh grade in 2027 to 2028.

**CDD fees:** North River Ranch carries Community Development District fees. Buyers should request the current CDD schedule during due diligence, as annual assessments typically add $1,500 to $3,000 per year on top of property taxes and HOA dues.

## Other Key Communities in the 34219 Market

Parrish is not all North River Ranch. Several other communities attract buyers in this ZIP code:

**Harrison Ranch:** One of the more established communities in Parrish, built from the mid-2000s onward. Single-family homes in the $340,000 to $480,000 range on larger lots than most new construction offers. Harrison Ranch has a community amenity center including a resort-style pool and sports courts. Schools include Virgil Mills Elementary and Buffalo Creek Middle School.

**Summerwoods:** A newer community with homes primarily in the $350,000 to $480,000 range from D.R. Horton and Lennar. Smaller lots than Harrison Ranch but newer construction and more modern floor plans.

**Bella Terra:** Established neighborhood with homes from the late 2000s and early 2010s. Prices run $330,000 to $430,000. Good value relative to comparable North River Ranch construction because the new home premium has been absorbed over time.

**Seaire:** A planned community that includes a lagoon-style amenity and waterfront lots. Pricing reflects the premium, with homes starting at $450,000 and going higher for lagoon access. For buyers specifically drawn to the lagoon lifestyle that SouthShore Bay in Wimauma popularized, Seaire offers a northern Manatee County alternative.

**Crosswind Point and Willow Bend:** Newer communities from Homes by WestBay and other Tampa Bay builders, targeting the $450,000 to $600,000 buyer segment with premium finishes and larger floor plans.

## School Zones in Parrish: What Buyers Need to Know

School zoning in Parrish is in active flux due to rapid population growth. The key schools and what you need to verify:

**Elementary:** Barbara A. Harvey Elementary serves North River Ranch. Virgil Mills Elementary serves parts of Parrish east of Fort Hamer Road. Additional schools are being planned as Manatee County responds to population growth with a $786 million school building initiative.

**Middle:** Buffalo Creek Middle School has been the primary middle school for Parrish. Harvey North River Ranch Middle School opened for the 2025 to 2026 school year within the community. Verify the specific middle school assignment for your address before making an offer, as the new school has changed some feeder patterns.

**High School: Parrish Community High School** opened in 2021 and serves the 34219 and 34221 ZIP codes. As of 2026, it is a relatively new school still building its academic profile and extracurricular programs. This is a meaningful difference from established high schools like Newsome or Bloomingdale in Hillsborough County, which carry decades of track record and alumni networks. Parrish Community High is accredited and growing, but buyers should visit the campus and review current state ratings before assuming it compares to the established schools they may be leaving behind.

Manatee County does not have an equivalent to Hillsborough County's Newsome High School zone in terms of established academic ranking and home value premium. This is relevant for buyers who are specifically prioritizing a well-established, top-ranked high school zone.

## Property Taxes: Manatee vs. Hillsborough

Property taxes in Parrish are assessed by Manatee County, not Hillsborough County. The millage rates differ:

- **Hillsborough County general millage (2026 combined rate):** approximately 20.0 to 21.5 mills depending on MSTU and service areas
- **Manatee County general millage (2026 combined rate):** approximately 19.5 to 20.5 mills depending on specific service area

The difference is not dramatic but can amount to $200 to $500 per year in lower taxes on a comparable-priced home. Florida's $50,000 homestead exemption applies in both counties, and the Save Our Homes 3% annual assessment cap provides identical protection for long-term owners.

Buyers relocating from Hillsborough County should request the specific Manatee County property tax estimate from the Manatee County Property Appraiser (manateepao.com) for the specific address being purchased.

## Flood Zones in Parrish

The 34219 ZIP code has varying flood zone profiles. The core residential areas of North River Ranch, Harrison Ranch, and Summerwoods are generally in FEMA Flood Zone X (minimal flood risk), which does not require flood insurance from most lenders. However:

- Properties near the Manatee River and its tributaries may fall in higher-risk flood zones
- The Terra Ceia Bay area on the western edge of the broader Parrish market carries coastal flood risk
- Recent development along the Fort Hamer Road corridor has affected drainage patterns in some areas

Always verify the flood zone on any specific address at msc.fema.gov before making an offer. Flood insurance in a Zone AE property can add $1,500 to $4,000 per year to your carrying costs.

## Commute Times from Parrish to Major Employment Centers

Parrish's location in northern Manatee County creates a longer commute profile to most Tampa Bay employment centers than Valrico or Brandon:

- **Downtown Tampa:** 40 to 55 minutes via US-301 north to I-275 or via the Selmon Expressway. Expect 50 minutes in normal traffic.
- **Brandon employment corridor (US-301/SR-60):** 25 to 35 minutes via US-301.
- **MacDill AFB:** 50 to 65 minutes. This is the most significant disadvantage for military families considering Parrish.
- **Bradenton/Sarasota employment:** 20 to 35 minutes. This is Parrish's commute advantage over Hillsborough communities.
- **Lakeland/Polk County:** 55 to 70 minutes via I-75 or US-301.

The I-75/Moccasin Wallow Road interchange in Parrish provides direct northbound and southbound interstate access, which improves commute predictability compared to surface road routes. The planned extension of Moccasin Wallow Road and ongoing improvements to Fort Hamer Road will improve connectivity as the community continues to grow.

## Is Parrish FL a Good Real Estate Investment in 2026?

For buyers evaluating Parrish as an investment, the case has two sides:

**Bullish factors:**
- Population growth is driving genuine demand. Manatee County has been one of Florida's fastest-growing counties.
- The I-75 corridor through Parrish is attracting logistics and light industrial development, creating local employment.
- Parrish Community High School is new and improving. A decade from now, it may carry a performance premium.
- The lagoon communities (Seaire) are still in early phases, and waterfront-adjacent amenity premiums tend to build over time.

**Cautionary factors:**
- Builder competition is real. When you resell a home in North River Ranch, you will compete with builders who have model homes, design centers, and incentive packages you cannot match.
- The 96.2% sale-to-list ratio and 53-day DOM indicate a healthy but not frantic market. Aggressive appreciation is not guaranteed.
- The CDD fees create a buyer pool filter. Some buyers specifically exclude CDD communities, which narrows your resale audience.
- Parrish's high school zone is unestablished relative to Newsome or Bloomingdale in Hillsborough County. That means less school-zone-driven demand from the highest-budget buyers.

For buy-and-hold investors targeting long-term appreciation and rental income, Parrish makes reasonable sense at current prices. For buyers expecting near-term equity gains or planning to sell in three to five years, the builder competition risk and modest current appreciation trend counsel caution.

## The VA Loan Advantage: What Makes It Different

The VA loan program, backed by the US Department of Veterans Affairs, offers three structural advantages over conventional and FHA financing that are especially relevant in a market like Valrico.

**No down payment required.** A VA loan with full entitlement requires no down payment regardless of purchase price. In a market where a typical Valrico 33596 home is priced around $450,000 to $470,000, eliminating the conventional 20 percent down requirement means keeping $90,000 to $94,000 in your pocket rather than tying it up in home equity from day one.

**No private mortgage insurance.** Conventional loans with less than 20 percent down require private mortgage insurance, which runs $100 to $200 per month on a Valrico-sized purchase. FHA loans require mortgage insurance for the life of the loan. VA loans have neither. This is a meaningful monthly savings that improves cash flow and effective purchasing power.

**Competitive rates.** VA mortgage rates in 2026 are running approximately 6.40 to 6.80 percent for well-qualified borrowers, roughly 25 to 40 basis points below conventional rates for comparable credit profiles. On a $450,000 loan, that rate differential saves $70 to $120 per month in principal and interest.

The VA loan also allows sellers to pay up to 4 percent of the sales price in concessions, which in the current Valrico market, where sellers are offering closing cost contributions routinely, can further reduce your out-of-pocket costs at closing.

## 2026 VA Loan Limits for Hillsborough County

VA loan limits govern the no-down-payment benefit for borrowers with full entitlement versus partial entitlement (meaning you have an existing VA loan active on another property).

For borrowers with full entitlement, the 2026 rules are uncomplicated: there is no limit. Full entitlement means you have never used your VA loan benefit or you have paid off a prior VA loan and had the entitlement restored. You can borrow as much as a lender will approve based on your income, credit, and the property appraisal, with no VA-imposed ceiling.

For borrowers with partial entitlement (remaining VA loan balance on another property), the calculation uses the FHFA conforming loan limit for Hillsborough County as the baseline. The 2026 conforming loan limit for Hillsborough County is $832,750. Partial entitlement borrowers must put a down payment on amounts above 25 percent of the county limit, but this applies to a small percentage of MacDill buyers.

If you are a first-time VA buyer or have restored entitlement, the practical limit is what your income and debt-to-income ratio support, not a government ceiling. At the median Valrico 33596 price of approximately $469,000, a qualified buyer with full VA entitlement needs zero down.

## MacDill AFB BAH Rates in 2026: What They Mean for Your Mortgage

Basic Allowance for Housing at MacDill AFB for 2026 reflects Tampa's cost of housing and military pay grade. BAH rates are set by the DoD Defense Travel Management Office and adjusted annually.

For the most common pay grades among MacDill buyers:

**E-5 with dependents:** $2,709 per month. At 6.5 percent on a $400,000 loan with zero down, principal and interest runs approximately $2,528 per month. Property taxes and insurance add roughly $500 to $600 per month on a $400,000 Valrico home. BAH at E-5 with dependents largely covers PITI on an appropriately priced Valrico 33594 home.

**O-3 with dependents:** $3,081 per month. At 6.5 percent on a $450,000 loan, principal and interest is approximately $2,844 per month. Taxes and insurance add approximately $550 to $650 per month. BAH at O-3 with dependents aligns with a Valrico 33596 purchase in the $430,000 to $460,000 range, particularly with seller-paid closing costs reducing the upfront burden.

VA lenders are permitted to gross BAH up to 125 percent of the actual allowance when calculating qualifying income, which improves the debt-to-income ratio. A lender grossing up E-5 BAH of $2,709 uses $3,386 as qualifying income from that source alone. This is specific to VA lending; conventional lenders do not gross up BAH the same way. It meaningfully increases the loan amount a service member can qualify for relative to conventional financing.

Confirm your specific BAH rate with the DoD's official BAH rate lookup at militaryone source.mil or through your installation's housing office, as rates vary by pay grade, dependency status, and military housing area.

## The VA Home Buying Process: Step by Step for MacDill Families

**Step 1: Get Your Certificate of Eligibility**

Your VA Certificate of Eligibility (COE) verifies to lenders that you have met the service requirements for the VA loan benefit. You can obtain it through the VA's eBenefits portal, through your lender (most VA-approved lenders can pull it online in minutes), or through Form 26-1880 if the electronic system cannot verify your service. Have your COE before you start shopping.

**Step 2: Get Pre-Approved with a VA-Experienced Lender**

Not all lenders are equally experienced with VA loans. Work with a lender who closes VA loans regularly and understands the VA appraisal process. VA appraisals use the VA's minimum property requirements (MPRs) to flag issues like roof condition, peeling paint, water intrusion, and safety items that conventional appraisals do not specifically address. A lender who is unfamiliar with VA MPRs can be caught flat-footed when an appraisal flags a condition that needs resolution before closing.

For MacDill families, the pre-approval should account for BAH income at the grossed-up amount, any base pay, special pays, and allowances the lender accepts as qualifying income.

**Step 3: Search in the Right ZIP Codes**

Valrico 33596 for the Newsome zone, larger homes, and established communities like River Hills and Buckhorn. Valrico 33594 for more affordable entry points in Bloomingdale, Twin Lakes, and Copper Ridge. Brandon 33511 for buyers who prioritize shorter commute over school zone. For the Brandon comparison, see the [Brandon FL Real Estate Market 2026](/blog/brandon-fl-real-estate-market-2026) guide.

**Step 4: Structure the Offer to Maximize VA Benefits**

In the current Valrico market, sellers are offering closing cost contributions routinely. A VA buyer can ask for up to 4 percent in seller concessions. On a $450,000 purchase, 4 percent is $18,000. That can cover the VA funding fee (which runs 1.25 to 3.3 percent of the loan amount for first use depending on down payment), prepaid items, and some discount points to reduce the rate.

The VA funding fee can be rolled into the loan balance, which preserves your cash. First-use borrowers with no down payment pay 2.3 percent. Subsequent use with no down payment is 3.6 percent. Borrowers with a service-connected disability rating of 10 percent or higher are exempt from the funding fee entirely.

**Step 5: Navigate the VA Appraisal**

The VA appraisal serves two functions: it confirms the property's value and it confirms the property meets VA minimum property requirements. Common VA MPR issues in Valrico include roof age and condition, peeling exterior paint on older homes, water heater location in the garage (must have a pan), and pool fencing if there are children. None of these are deal-killers if identified early and addressed. Work with an agent familiar with VA transactions who can flag likely MPR issues before the appraisal is ordered.

**Step 6: Close and File Homestead Exemption**

If you close by December 31 and file your Florida homestead exemption by March 1, 2027, you qualify for the 2027 property tax savings from Amendment 3's newly expanded homestead exemption. For a Valrico home assessed at $450,000, that is approximately $1,100 per year in annual savings starting 2027. Active duty military stationed in Florida are eligible for homestead exemption if they intend to establish permanent Florida residency.

## Common VA Loan Misconceptions Among Military Buyers

**Misconception: Sellers will not accept VA offers.**
This was a valid concern in the 2021 to 2022 seller's market, when sellers had multiple all-cash offers and could be selective. In the fall 2026 Valrico market, with extended days on market and sellers actively competing for qualified buyers, VA offers are accepted routinely. A buyer with a pre-approval letter, flexibility on possession date, and a motivated agent resolves most seller concerns about VA transactions.

**Misconception: VA loans take longer to close.**
VA loans with experienced lenders close in 30 to 45 days, the same as conventional financing. The appraisal turnaround is typically 7 to 14 business days for Hillsborough County. The COE can be obtained in minutes online. Delays are caused by inexperienced lenders, not by the VA program itself.

**Misconception: VA loans are only for first-time buyers.**
VA entitlement can be restored and reused multiple times over a military career. A service member who bought and sold a home near a prior duty station can restore full entitlement and use the VA benefit again at MacDill without any restriction.

## Flood Zones and Insurance in Valrico vs. South Tampa

One of the strongest arguments for Valrico over South Tampa or waterfront properties is flood zone classification. Most of Valrico sits in FEMA Zone X, which carries no mandatory flood insurance requirement and minimal flood risk. Properties in South Tampa, Hyde Park, Davis Islands, and along Tampa Bay carry Zone AE or VE classifications with mandatory flood insurance that can add $2,000 to $5,000 or more annually to housing costs.

For a service member using BAH to cover housing costs, flood insurance significantly affects the affordability calculation. A Zone AE property in South Tampa with $4,000 annual flood insurance adds $333 per month to the effective housing cost, which can push total PITI above BAH at most pay grades. Valrico's Zone X classification avoids this entirely for the vast majority of homes.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Can I use a VA loan to buy in a Valrico HOA community?**
Yes. VA loans are compatible with HOA communities. The VA does not restrict purchases in communities with homeowners associations. River Hills Country Club, Buckhorn, Copper Ridge, and other Valrico communities with HOAs are all eligible for VA financing. The lender and appraiser will review HOA documents as part of the loan process, but the HOA structure itself is not an obstacle.

**What if I am not yet at MacDill but I am PCS-ing there in the next six months?**
You can begin the pre-approval process and start working with an agent before you arrive. Many MacDill buyers complete virtual tours, submit offers, and go under contract before physically arriving in Tampa. Barrett works with remote buyers regularly and can represent you effectively throughout the transaction if you are not yet local.

**Does my VA loan benefit change if I retire after buying in Valrico?**
No. Your VA loan benefit does not expire and your entitlement is not affected by retirement status. If you buy a home in Valrico on VA financing while active duty and later retire in the area, the loan terms remain unchanged. Florida's homestead exemption, which you are entitled to as a Florida resident, also applies equally to veterans and retirees.

**What is the VA funding fee and how do I pay it?**
The VA funding fee is a one-time charge to the federal government that helps sustain the VA loan program. For a first-time VA buyer with no down payment in 2026, the fee is 2.3 percent of the loan amount. On a $450,000 loan, that is $10,350. It can be paid at closing or rolled into the loan balance. Borrowers with a service-connected disability of 10 percent or higher, Purple Heart recipients, and certain surviving spouses are exempt from the funding fee entirely.
